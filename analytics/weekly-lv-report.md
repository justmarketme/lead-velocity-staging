# Lead Velocity weekly (internal) — template and SQL per section

Generated by W14 on Sunday 23:00, delivered Monday 07:00 with the weekly memo. Form: numbers + 3 insights + 1 recommendation (Kaushik). Nothing is reported without a decision attached. Reader: Jonathan and KG; read on a phone in under 3 minutes. Run `select facts.w14_lv_payload()` (`analytics/W14-lv.sql`); Sonnet words the insights from the seeds and may not add a number that is not in the payload. Every number must resolve to `knowledge/metrics.md`.

## Template

**This week in one line:** (Sonnet, one sentence from the payload: delivered vs committed, margin, the one thing to do.)

**1. Funnel per adviser** (this week | last week | target) — `facts.v_w14_lv_funnel`
Sign-ups -> qualified and replied -> booked -> attended -> good fit. Beside it the rates M10, M11, M03, M04 and M02 with their targets from `facts.v_watchlist_*` (`watchlist` in the payload).

**2. Margin vs the pricing model** — `facts.v_w14_lv_margin`
Per active cycle: delivered of committed, replacements used of cap, margin so far, projected margin, margin at the stress cost per lead (>= 30% target, 3.5), days left, extension. Flag red if margin at stress < 30%.

**3. Renewal risk** — `facts.v_w14_lv_renewal`
Green / amber / red per adviser with the reasons in words. Amber or red becomes a call on the to-do list; report unopened two weeks = Jonathan calls.

**4. Cost per attended meeting per creative** — `facts.v_w14_lv_creative`
Per ad: spend, qualified, attended, good fit, cost per attended meeting, cost per good-fit meeting, quality score, "enough data" flag (5 or more ratings). Ads below the flag are listed but never ranked.

**5. Insights (exactly 3)** — seeds from `facts.v_w14_lv_insight_seeds` in priority order: biggest funnel leak against target; best and worst creative; margin; renewal risk. Each insight = number + comparison + "so what" in one sentence. If fewer than 3 seeds have enough data, write "not enough data yet" for the rest; do not pad.

**6. Recommendation (exactly 1)** — `facts.v_w14_lv_recommendation_seed` (highest priority kill/scale candidate: escalate > pause > review > scale > capacity). Written as a Do-today-style line with the number it moves and an Approve button (creates the `ops.proposals` row). If no candidate: "No change this week" and the reason (gate not open, or all within limits).

**7. Terms you'll see this week:** only when a new term appears (6A2 item 5).

## Rules
- Never selects policies or close rate (`facts.fact_broker_roi`): FAIS, 3.7.
- Per-adviser rows only; no averages across advisers (6A2 "deliberately not copied").
- Spend is ex VAT in tables; margin includes VAT on media.
- W33 judge checks: numbers reconcile to `facts.v_watchlist_*`, no number without a dictionary entry, exactly 3 insights and 1 recommendation.

## Broker weekly (separate report)
`analytics/W14-broker.sql`: `facts.w14_broker_payload(broker_id)` produces sections 1 to 8 of 4.10a plus the six `broker_weekly` WhatsApp variables (`payload.whatsapp`). `facts.w14_reconcile(broker_id)` re-computes the cycle line through a second path (`facts.v_cycle_progress`, the console's) and checks the to-do count against `fact_broker_day`, the disposition mix, the one-liner, and that the payload holds no cost, creative, jargon, other-adviser id or lead key in the WhatsApp text. `facts.w14_hold(broker_id)` true means do not send, alert Jonathan with the failing check names.
