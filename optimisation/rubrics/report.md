# Rubric: reports (broker weekly, mid-cycle, cycle-end; Lead Velocity weekly) — faculty `broker`

Sample: every report generated by W14 on Sunday 23:00 (before the 07:00 Monday send) and every renewal offer (W19). If a report fails a `critical` or `high` rule it is held and an alert is raised (4.6 W14: "numbers don't reconcile -> hold and alert"). Owner: `analytics-reporter` and `broker-success`. Sources: 4.10a, 6A2.

| ID | Rule | Pass test | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | **Numbers reconcile** | Every figure in the WhatsApp 6-liner, the portal Reports tab, the email and the PDF equals the same figure recomputed from `facts` for that broker and week (tolerance 0) | critical (hold the report) |
| R-02 | **Every number is in the metric dictionary** | Each metric shown resolves to an entry in `/knowledge/metrics.md` with a target | high |
| R-03 | **No banned words** | None of: guaranteed (use "committed"), hot/warm leads, estimated leads, best/cheapest, financial advice, "appointments" as the unit sold; no CPL, EMQ, CAPI, attribution or spend on a broker surface | high |
| R-04 | **One ask** | Exactly one ask with one button; none if there is nothing to act on | high |
| R-05 | **Order and shape** | One-liner, progress, meetings and to-dos, quality in his words, what you'll notice (only when true), ROI view (only if he entered a close rate), one ask, cycle line; same order every week | medium |
| R-06 | **Targets and last week beside every number** | value, target and last week present | medium |
| R-07 | **Length and level** | WhatsApp <= 6 lines and no lead's full name; < 150 words before the first table; Grade 7 | medium |
| R-08 | **Privacy** | Aggregates only on WhatsApp; no other broker's data; policies written appears only in his own ROI view and never in any fee or ranking | critical |
| R-09 | **No invented projection** | "Tracking to N policies" only if he entered a close rate | high |
| R-10 | **Internal reports** | Lead Velocity weekly: margin, renewal risk and funnel reconcile to `facts.fact_cycle`; a "what this means for the business" line is present and costs a number | medium |
