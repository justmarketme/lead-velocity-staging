# Rubric: new creative (ad stills, Reels, stories, intro cards, end-frames) — faculty `media`

Sample: every creative asset first seen in the last 24 hours (`ad_creatives` / asset manifest), before and after Meta approval. Owners: `creative-strategist` (copy), `visual-producer` (render), `compliance-qa` (sign-off). Sources: 2.1.3, 2.1.5, 2.1.8, 3.5a, 4D.2, 4D.4b, 6B.4, 6B.8.

| ID | Rule | Pass test | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| K-01 | **No second-person assertions about the viewer's finances, debts, family, health or ethnicity** | "Most bonds are bigger than most work cover" passes; "Your bond is bigger than your cover" fails | critical (Meta policy and compliance) |
| K-02 | **No product, insurer, premium, cover amount or broker named** | Text scan plus read of on-image text | critical |
| K-03 | **Banned words** | None of: guaranteed sales, hot/warm leads, best/cheapest cover, estimated leads, financial advice, any rand premium or cover figure | high |
| K-04 | **Never "you should"; never best/cheapest** | Voice rule | high |
| K-05 | **AI imagery labelled; no fake people as clients or advisers; no invented statistics or testimonials** | "AI-generated imagery" label on stills with people; every number has a source on file | critical if a fabricated claim; high if label missing |
| K-06 | **Brand tokens** | Colours, type, logo clear space from `/brand/tokens.json`; no off-token colour | medium |
| K-07 | **Legible and accessible** | Text contrast >= 4.5:1 (amber text only on charcoal; charcoal #2A1B02 on amber), on-image text in the safe zone, captions on video | medium |
| K-08 | **Plain reading level** | Grade 5-7 | medium |
| K-09 | **One idea, one call to action** | Concept matches its angle tag; a single CTA | low |
| K-10 | **Hook in the first 3 seconds (video)** | Subject and promise visible before second 3 | low (advisory; hook rate is judged from data) |
| K-11 | **Consistency with the funnel** | The promise in the ad is what the page and the first WhatsApp message say | high if it over-promises |
