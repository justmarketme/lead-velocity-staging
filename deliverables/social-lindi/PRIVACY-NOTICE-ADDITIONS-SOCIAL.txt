2. ADD TO "WHAT WE COLLECT" (after the line "Your chats with our WhatsApp assistant.")
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your comments and private messages to us on Facebook and Instagram. When you comment on one of our posts or ads, or send
our Page or Instagram account a message, Meta passes it to us. We use the words to answer you. We do not keep the words
of your comment or message, or your name. We keep a coded record that something arrived, what kind of question it was,
what we sent back, and whether a person from our team took over. If you post personal details in a public comment, such
as a phone number, ID number, email address or something about your health, we hide that comment to protect you.

5. ADD TO "HOW LONG WE KEEP IT" (a new line)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Records of comments and private messages on Facebook and Instagram: 30 days. After 30 days we delete what Lindi sent
and the links to your comment or message. We keep only a coded marker so that we never message you again if you said
STOP, never contact you if you told us you are under 18, and leave a conversation alone if a person from our team took
it over. Comments you post stay on Facebook or Instagram until you delete them, or until we hide them.

6. ADD TO THE AUTOMATED-ASSISTANT PARAGRAPH (after "Type "person" at any time to reach a human.")
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On Facebook and Instagram, Lindi answers in her own name as "SortMyCover's automated assistant". In public she posts
one short reply, with our WhatsApp number. In a private message she can answer quick questions about the call and
show you how to book on WhatsApp. She never gives advice, prices or quotes, and she never books you from Facebook or
Instagram: booking only happens on WhatsApp, where we ask for your consent first. Ask for a person at any time. If a
person from our team replies to you, Lindi stops for that conversation.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ADD TO "STOPPING MESSAGES" (after "We stop at once and tell the adviser.")
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On Facebook or Instagram, reply STOP to any message from us. We will not message you there again, and we keep a coded
record of that so it lasts.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. ADD A SHORT "CHILDREN" LINE (if the notice has no age section yet)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Our service is for adults aged 18 and older. If you tell us on Facebook or Instagram that you are under 18, we reply
once to say so and do not contact you again.
