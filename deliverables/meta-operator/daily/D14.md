How to check an adviser is licensed.

1. Ask for the adviser's full name and the FSP number.
2. Search it on the FSCA website (fsca.co.za) or call the FSCA toll-free on 0800 110 443.
3. Check that the name is listed under that FSP number, and check the status shown.

The FSCA also says authorised financial services providers must clearly display their authorisation status in their documentation (FSCA, 29 September 2026).
