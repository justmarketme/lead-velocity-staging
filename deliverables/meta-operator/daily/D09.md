A new life policy comes with a 31-day window, with some exceptions.

Under the Policyholder Protection Rules (Long-term Insurance), Rule 4, a new policy can be cancelled within 31 days of receiving the policy information, as long as no benefit has been paid or claimed and no insured event has happened.
