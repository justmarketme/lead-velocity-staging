## Facebook text (~640 chars)

```text
New life policy? There is a 31-day window.

It is called a cooling-off period. Under South Africa's policyholder protection rules, a new individual life policy can usually be cancelled within 31 days of receiving the policy information, as long as no claim has been made (National Financial Ombud, 2024).

Premiums paid are then refunded, less the cost of any cover already enjoyed.

The point is not to cancel. It is time to read the contract calmly and ask questions.
```
