## Sources (all checked 6 Oct 2026)

| Claim | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Non-provisional individuals file 13 Jul–23 Oct 2026 | SARS, [Filing Season](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/filing-season/) ("13 July – 23 October 2026") | page updated 21 Sep 2026 |
| An employer's contribution to an insurer for an employee's insurance benefits is a taxable fringe benefit reflected under code 3801 on the IRP5, except where the policy "relates to an event arising solely out of and in the course of employment" (§4.10, paras 2(k), 12C). Hence "can include", never "are taxable" | SARS, [Guide for Employers in respect of Fringe Benefits](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Guides/PAYE-GEN-01-G02-Guide-for-Employers-in-respect-of-Fringe-Benefits-External-Guide.pdf) (PAYE-GEN-01-G02), §4.10 | Revision 16, 8 Apr 2026 (PDF read 6 Oct 2026) |
| Code 3801 is used for several fringe benefits, not only insurance: assets acquired below value (§4.1), right of use of an asset (§4.3), meals (§4.5), free or cheap services (§4.7), debt subsidies (§4.9) and insurance contributions (§4.10). Hence "can point to cover through work, but not always" | Same guide, §§4.1, 4.3, 4.5, 4.7, 4.9, 4.10 | Revision 16, 8 Apr 2026 |
| Risk benefits provided through a pension or provident fund are a "defined contribution component", valued as part of the employer's fund contribution under paras 2(l) and 12D, not under §4.10 | Same guide, §6 | Revision 16, 8 Apr 2026 |
| Before advice, a provider must obtain information about the client's needs and objectives and financial situation | FAIS General Code of Conduct (BN 80 of 2003, as amended by BN 706 of 26 Jun 2020), s8(1)(a); consolidated text via [Masthead](https://www.masthead.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/BN-80-of-2003-FAIS-GCOC-June-2020.pdf) | 26 Jun 2020 (latest amendment) |
| Of the three papers, only the IRP5 is on SARS's list of filing documents (hence "the IRP5 in that pile", and no "same pile" claim) | SARS, [Supporting Documents for Filing](https://www.sars.gov.za/types-of-tax/personal-income-tax/filing-season/supporting-documents-for-filing/) lists the IRP5/IT3(a), interest, medical, retirement annuity and other certificates; no bond statement or policy schedule | page updated 4 Nov 2025, read 6 Oct 2026 |
| Policy schedules list cover in place (round 3: unsourced "issued by each insurer" claim and "Issued by: The insurer" note removed; card 3 "what is still owed on the home" reduced to a plain label) | Definition only; no claim about beneficiaries (removed: nominations are recorded separately with the insurer, NFO, [Policy beneficiaries must be kept up to date](https://nfosa.co.za/policy-beneficiaries-must-be-kept-up-to-date-with-insurer/), 5 Feb 2025) | — |
