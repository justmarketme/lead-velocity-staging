## Facebook text (~650 chars)

```text
Two free places to take a financial complaint.

1. The National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO). For complaints about insurers, banks and credit. It replaced four older ombuds on 1 March 2024. Free for consumers.

2. The FAIS Ombud. For complaints about financial advisers and intermediaries. Also free.

The first step for both: complain to the company or adviser in writing. The FAIS Ombud gives the adviser six weeks to resolve it before taking the case.

Every adviser on a SortMyCover call shares a name and FSP number first, so there is always a name to go to.

Pick a time for a free 30-minute call with a licensed adviser: sortmycover.co.za

SortMyCover gives no financial advice, product comparisons or premium quotes.
```
