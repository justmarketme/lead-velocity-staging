| A provider must give the client its business or trade name and details of the financial services it is authorised to provide under its licence "at the earliest reasonable opportunity". This holds for both kinds of provider: s5(a) and (d) for providers other than direct marketers, and s15(1)(a) and (b) for direct marketers (phone, internet or app-based). The 30-day written confirmation is in s5 only, so the copy does not use it | FAIS General Code of Conduct (BN 80 of 2003, as amended by BN 706 of 26 Jun 2020), s5 (non-direct-marketers) and s15(1) (direct marketers); consolidated text via [Masthead](https://www.masthead.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/BN-80-of-2003-FAIS-GCOC-June-2020.pdf) | 26 Jun 2020 (latest amendment), re-read 6 Oct 2026 |

Removed in round 1: "Every adviser on a SortMyCover call shares a name and FSP number first" (internal claim, no public source); replaced by the General Code s5 duty.
Removed in round 2: "confirm them in writing within 30 days if given verbally" (s5 only; direct marketers under s15 have no 30-day duty, and a phone-based adviser can be a direct marketer); "So there is always a name to take a complaint to" (turns a legal duty into a promise; an unlicensed operator gives no name). Paragraph 4 now states only the duty both s5 and s15 share, for licensed firms, and ties it to the "company or adviser first" step in paragraph 3.

Two free places to take a financial complaint.

1. The National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO). For complaints about insurers, banks and credit providers. It replaced four older ombuds on 1 March 2024. Free for consumers.

2. The FAIS Ombud. For complaints about financial advisers and intermediaries. Also free.

The first step for both: take the complaint to the company or adviser first. The FAIS Ombud recommends doing this in writing, and gives the adviser six weeks to resolve it.

Under the FAIS General Code of Conduct, a licensed adviser's firm must give its business name and licence details at the earliest reasonable opportunity. That is the firm a complaint goes to first.

Pick a time for a free 30-minute call with a licensed adviser: sortmycover.co.za
