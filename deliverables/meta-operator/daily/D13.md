Stokvel, burial society, life cover: three different jobs.

A stokvel is a group that pools money for a shared goal. The National Stokvel Association of South Africa (NASASA) lists six common types, from savings and grocery stokvels to investment clubs. In a rotational stokvel, members take turns to receive the pot.

A burial society is one of those types. Members pool money for funeral costs and support during bereavement.

Life cover is a policy that pays out when the insured person dies. With a valid nomination recorded by the insurer, the money goes straight to the beneficiary (National Financial Ombud, 2025).
