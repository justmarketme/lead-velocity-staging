## Facebook text (~690 chars)

```text
Myth: the nomination form decides who gets paid.

For a retirement fund, it doesn't. Section 37C of the Pension Funds Act tells the fund's trustees to share a death benefit fairly among the member's dependants. The nomination form is a guide they must look at, not the final word.

What do trustees look at? Who depended on the member, and how much. Many families support more than one household, so this matters.

If no dependant is found within 12 months and nobody was nominated, the benefit goes to the estate.
```
