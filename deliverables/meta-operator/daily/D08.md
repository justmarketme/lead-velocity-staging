## Facebook text (~1,200 chars)

```text
Myth: the nomination form decides who gets paid.

For a retirement fund registered under the Pension Funds Act, it doesn't. Section 37C tells the fund's trustees to share a death benefit fairly among the member's dependants and any nominees. The nomination form is a guide, not the final word.

What do trustees look at? Who depended on the member, and how much. In 2025 the Constitutional Court held that dependency is judged at the date of death and that the fund must investigate properly. In that case, claims came from two households.

If no dependant is traced within 12 months and nobody was nominated, the benefit is paid into the estate (or, if no estate is reported to the Master, into the Guardian's Fund or an unclaimed benefit fund).

A life policy held outside a retirement fund works differently. With a valid nomination recorded by the insurer, the money is paid directly to the beneficiary (National Financial Ombud, 2025). A licensed adviser can explain the difference on a free 30-minute call, booked for a time that suits.
```
