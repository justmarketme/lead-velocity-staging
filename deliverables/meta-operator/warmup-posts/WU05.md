## Facebook text (as scheduled/published after QA, 2026-10-06; 627 chars)

```text
Who gives the advice? Not SortMyCover.

How SortMyCover works, in three lines:

1. A person learns about the cover gap and, if they want, asks for a call.
2. SortMyCover helps book a free 30-minute call with a licensed adviser, for a time that suits, then steps back.
3. The adviser gives the advice. SortMyCover does not.

Advisers pay SortMyCover a flat fee for each 30-day cycle. The fee is the same whether or not anyone buys. SortMyCover never takes commission.

Pick a time for a free 30-minute call with a licensed adviser: sortmycover.co.za

SortMyCover gives no financial advice, product comparisons or premium quotes.
```
