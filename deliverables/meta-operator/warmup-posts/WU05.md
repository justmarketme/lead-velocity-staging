## Facebook text (592 chars)

```text
Who gives the advice? Not SortMyCover.

How SortMyCover works, in three lines:

1. A person learns about the cover gap and, if they want, asks for a call.
2. SortMyCover helps book a free 30-minute call with a licensed adviser, then steps back.
3. The adviser gives the advice. SortMyCover does not.

Advisers pay Lead Velocity the same flat fee for each 30-day cycle, whether or not anyone buys. SortMyCover never takes commission.

Questions about the free 30-minute call? Send us a message.
```
