## Facebook text (622 chars)

```text
That line on the payslip that says group life, death benefit or risk benefit: what does it usually mean?

It is usually a lump sum paid to chosen beneficiaries if a member dies while employed, worked out as a multiple of yearly salary.

What it usually does not do: carry on after the job ends, or grow when a new bond or a baby arrives.

Three questions many people take to HR: How is the amount worked out? What happens if the job ends? Who is the beneficiary?
```
