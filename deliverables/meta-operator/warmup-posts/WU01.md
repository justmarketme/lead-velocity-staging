## Facebook text (674 chars)

```text
Cover through work and a family's costs are often two different numbers.

Many employers give staff group life cover. It usually pays a set multiple of yearly salary, often a few times salary.

A family's big costs can add up to more: the bond or rent, everyday living costs, school fees, other debts, and relatives who depend on the income.

The difference is called a life cover gap. It is common, and it can quietly grow as a bond grows or a baby arrives.

Only a licensed adviser can say what suits one family.
```
