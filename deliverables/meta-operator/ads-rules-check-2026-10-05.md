# Meta / FAIS / POPIA check for SortMyCover ads (2026-10-05)

Question: does anything require broker or FSP details on the SortMyCover Page or in its ads? **No.**

| Topic | Finding | Effect on us | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta financial-advertiser verification (licence + beneficiary/payer) | Enforced in UK, AU, IN, TW, SG, TH (third-party sources say about 38 countries in 2026). ZA is not on any list found. Meta never requires an FSP number on a Page | Nothing to show. Keep CIPC documents ready in case Meta asks; then appeal, then the standby SMC Page (never a broker's Page) | Medium-high (no official full list) |
| Special Ad Category: Financial products and services | Includes insurance. Mandatory for the US (since 21 Jan 2025); no source says ZA | Full targeting stays for ZA. Design so the creative still works if Meta enforces it (18–65+, no lookalikes) | Medium |
| Meta Lead Ads terms | Lead data can't be sold; it may be shared only for the purpose stated at collection. The form must link the advertiser's own privacy policy. No questions on health, income, debt, net worth or credit without Meta's permission | Form notice: "a licensed FSP will contact you". Link the SMC privacy policy. **Flag: the qualifying "budget" question (R750–R1,250 / R1,250+) may count as a financial question.** Ask it on WhatsApp, not in the instant form | High / medium |
| FAIS GCoC s14 (advertising) | Binds FSPs only. Factual and promotional material is not advice (s1(3)(a)) | No FSP number on the Page or ads while SMC gives no advice | High |
| *Raspberry Academy v Oaksure* (14 Apr 2026) | Pay as a % of premium, or contingent on a policy being sold = unlicensed intermediary | Our flat 30-day-cycle fee is the right model; never tie the fee to sales | High |
| "R250 referral cap" | **Not verified.** The only figure found is GCoC s3A, R1,000/yr immaterial interest | Practitioner to confirm | Low |
| POPIA s18 / s69, several brokers | A category of recipients is lawful ("a licensed FSP from our panel"). Consent must be specific; s69(4) means the sender identifies themselves | Generic consent workable; the broker's first WhatsApp identifies them. Fresh consent for each new product line. Consider a public panel list on the site | Medium |

Sources: Meta financial-services policy (transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/financial-services/), Meta Lead Ads Terms (facebook.com/legal/leadgen/tos), Moonstone on the Raspberry judgment, FAIS GCoC BN 80 of 2003, Information Regulator Direct Marketing Guidance Note.
