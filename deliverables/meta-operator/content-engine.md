## 3. Top 10 principles (ranked; each tied to a grade)

1. **Watch time first** (A). Hook text visible at 0.0 s, payoff promised in frame 1 and delivered by second 5, a cut every 2–3 s, burned-in captions (sound-off feeds).
2. **Built to be sent** (A). Each post answers one question a person would forward to a partner or sibling. Judge posts on **sends per reach**, not likes.
3. **Honest curiosity gap** (A/B). Line 1 opens a specific gap; the post closes it. No withholding, no "you won't believe" (Meta demotes it; credibility drops).
4. **One useful, surprising fact per post** (B). Usefulness and surprise drive sharing without fear (Berger & Milkman).
5. **Daily feed post** (B). Reach per post rises with frequency; one post a day at 18:00 SAST.
6. **Reels for reach, carousels for depth** (B). At least 2 Reels and 2 carousels a week; stills for current facts.
7. **One fact, four cuts** (A constraint, C method). Weekly pillar fact → still, carousel, Reel, Story frame. Every cut is re-written (originality rule).
8. **Plain words, one idea** (B, Unbounce Grade 5–7). Short sentences, third person, no jargon without a one-line definition.
9. **No bait, fast replies** (A/B). Never ask for likes, comments, tags or shares. Reply in < 15 min; replies correlate with +21% engagement.
10. **Test one variable a week** (B/C; Loomer, Kane). Change hook *or* format *or* time, never two. Decide on 14 days of data.

**Which of my five would do this?** Andromeda (distinct angles daily), Loomer (one-variable tests), Unbounce (Grade 5–7), SA long-runners (educational, gap-framed), Ethos (teach the gap, don't sell). All ten pass that test.

## 4. Hook formulas (first line ≤ 9 words; all compliant)

| Formula | Example | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Question + instant answer | "Who gives the advice? Not SortMyCover." | Gap opened and closed in 2 s |
| Number + concrete thing | "3 papers worth finding before a cover call." | Specific, saveable |
| Myth, then fact | "Myth: the nomination form decides who gets paid." | Surprise (B) |
| Life moment | "Leaving a job? One question for HR first." | Category entry point, no "you/your" |
| Sourced statistic | "94.1% of death claims were paid in 2025." | Numeric hook (+31% in finance, 4D.4a) |
| How to check | "How to check an adviser is licensed." | Utility + trust |
| Plain definition | "5 words that come up on a cover call." | Removes fear of jargon |
| Rule people don't know | "New life policy? There is a 31-day window." | Information gap with a real payoff |

**Banned hooks:** "your cover is too low", "are you protected?", "you won't believe", "comment CALL", "tag someone who…", death or fear images, prices or cover amounts, insurer/product/broker names.

## 7. Engagement loop

1. **Comment** → one public reply in < 15 min (≤ 2 sentences, Grade 5–7, no advice), then one private reply (once) with `sortmycover.co.za` (or `{{WA_LINK}}` once live).
2. **DM** → answer from the FAQ, say SortMyCover gives no advice, share the booking link. Never ask for an ID or phone number in Messenger/IG (no consent yet, POPIA).
3. **Sensitive** (illness, death, claims) → human only, private only.
4. **Every question seen 3 times** → next week's post (profile-kit §4 #7).
