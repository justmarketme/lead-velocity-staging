# SortMyCover Page + Instagram: optimisation research → spec

Status: v1 · 5 Oct 2026 · Owner: creative-strategist for meta-operator · Asked for by the orchestrator. This is a new topic, so it does not re-run or re-rank the research already in the master prompt.
Scope: what top Meta partners recommend for converting and trusted Pages and IG profiles, each claim checked against Meta's own docs or large benchmark data, then filtered through FAIS, POPIA, Meta personal-attributes rules and master prompt 2.1.

**Grades.** **A** = Meta's own documentation, policy or platform behaviour. **B** = large independent dataset (Socialinsider, Rival IQ, Sprout, Hootsuite) or a Meta executive's on-record statement as reported. **C** = agency opinion, small or unsourced numbers.

## 3. Page optimisation spec for SortMyCover (ranked by likely effect × evidence)

Fits FAIS (no advice, comparisons or quotes), POPIA s69 (no unsolicited marketing, no numbers collected in DMs before consent) and Meta rules (no "you/your" attribute claims, 18+, no engagement bait).

| Rank | Action | Grade | Owner · when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Weekly Reel, original, captions burned in, hook in the first 2 s, payoff by 5 s.** Built to be *sent* (a clear "how it works" a person forwards to a partner) | B | visual-producer renders; Jonathan posts · from warm-up day 3 |
| 2 | **No engagement bait, ever. One CTA: "Send us a message."** Meta demotes bait; 4.14 already bans it | A | everyone · now |
| 3 | **Earn the "Very responsive" badge: ≥ 90 % DMs answered, < 15 min, 07:00–22:00.** Off hours: away message (NEW, compliance-qa): `Thanks for your message. SortMyCover replies from 07:00. We don't give advice ourselves.` Spam goes to Spam, finished threads to Done | A | Jonathan/KG, then W31 · from Page live |
| 4 | **One action button, matched to what is live**: Send message → Send WhatsApp message → (domain) Learn more | A | Jonathan · now, then at G4 #8 |
| 5 | **Pin for trust.** FB pins WU05. IG pins WU05 + the R01 Reel + the FAQ carousel | A / C | Jonathan · end of warm-up week |
| 6 | **At least 1 carousel a week** (FAQ / how-it-works). Highest-engagement IG format, and saveable | B | creative-strategist → visual-producer · weekly |
| 7 | **Cadence 3–4 posts a week after warm-up**, same post to FB + IG, 4–5 specific hashtags, 18:00 SAST, then test 07:00 after 4 weeks | B / C | Jonathan schedules in Business Suite |
| 8 | **Complete the profile, finish the leftovers**: username @sortmycover, About = DISC-FULL-v1, IG name with "Life cover & insurance, explained", category Education, 4 Highlights | A (fields) / C (lift) | Jonathan · G2 |
| 9 | **Reviews off until real calls exist.** Then switch on, never solicit with incentives, never show an adviser's name | A | Jonathan · after first real calls |
| 10 | **Daily Story = share that day's post.** It feeds Highlights and costs nothing extra | C | Jonathan · warm-up week |

**Measure (Page Insights + IG Insights, weekly):** Reel 3-s view rate and average watch time; sends per reach; Page visits → messages started; median DM response time and badge state; profile visits → Message taps. Rule: change one variable a week (Loomer method). Make no decisions before 14 days of data (pre-mortem #13).

**Not adopted:** Live video, Groups seeding, review/testimonial asks, comment-to-win contests, trending-audio Reels (licensed-music risk on a brand account, and our captions carry the message), Meta Verified (spend).
