# search-findability-lead: Phase 0 holding site

The static SortMyCover holding site is built in `landing/holding/` and not deployed (waits on GATE-DOMAINS and GATE-DNS). It has six pages (home, About, How we make money, Privacy structure, Complaints, 404), one stylesheet using the brand tokens, a theme-aware favicon, manifest, robots, sitemap and a Hostinger `.htaccess` (HTTPS, .com and www redirects, security headers, caching, staging noindex). The home page carries Organization and FAQPage JSON-LD, the verbatim disclosure, three trust chips and no data capture. `deploy.md` is the GoDaddy/Hostinger/Search Console/GBP/Meta-verification runbook and `README.md` holds the brand-SERP checklist and the five Phase 1 YMYL page briefs. Total is about 29.8 KB (about 3.4 KB gzipped for index plus CSS), no images or JavaScript. Open items: placeholders to fill, icon/OG PNGs from visual-producer, privacy text from contracts-drafter, and the human decisions listed in the hand-off.
