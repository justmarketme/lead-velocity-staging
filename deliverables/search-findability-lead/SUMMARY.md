# search-findability-lead: holding site, learn pages and brand SERP plan

The static SortMyCover holding site in `landing/holding/` (not deployed, waiting on GATE-DOMAINS and GATE-DNS) has six pages (home, About, How we make money, Privacy structure, Complaints, 404), a shared stylesheet, favicon, manifest, robots, sitemap, a Hostinger `.htaccess` and the `deploy.md` runbook. It now also has `learn/`: a hub plus the five YMYL educational pages (life cover gap, 30-minute adviser call, life events, payslip cover line, how SortMyCover works and makes money). Each is static, no data capture, 330 to 400 words, about Grade 5 to 6 on Flesch-Kincaid, one H1, with Article, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, canonical, OG tags, internal links, the standard header, footer and disclosure, and a "Check back soon" slot with a commented quiz-link CTA. They pass an HTML parse check and the 3.5a banned-word scan. The sitemap lists 11 URLs and the home footer has a Learn link. `serp-plan.md` sets the target SERP for the four brand queries, the launch-day indexing checklist (Search Console, GBP category guidance, NAP), Core Web Vitals budgets, weekly branded-search monitoring, the Google Ads brand-term-only conditions (SA verification is an ASSUMPTION to re-check), and staging rules with the GATE-DOMAINS defer path and cutover SEO steps.
