## 4. Questions for the practitioner (add to `practitioner-brief.md`)

1. Does an obligation to replace no-show leads (capped, free, never linked to policies) weaken the FAIS position compared with discretionary replacement? Our default: no — it is a delivery-quality term, not pay for results. Alternative: keep replacements as a capped credit rather than a lead.
2. Is the counting rule (a replaced lead stops counting; its replacement counts) safe under Raspberry [25]–[27]? Default: yes — the fee never changes.
