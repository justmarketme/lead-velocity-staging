BEFORE DRAFTING — RESEARCH (do this first, cite sources in a separate research memo)
1. Read and summarise Raspberry Academy (Pty) Ltd v Oaksure Financial Services (Pty) Ltd (2025/219635) [2026] ZAGPJHC 388 (14 April 2026), Johannesburg High Court. Key point: a "Lead Referral Agreement" that labelled the referrer a "marketing agent and lead referrer only" was held to be, in substance, unlicensed intermediary services under the FAIS Act, and therefore unenforceable. The court looked past the label to the operative terms. The clearest indicator was that remuneration was paid only where a lead entered into a policy (success-based, causally linked to policy conclusion).
2. Check the FAIS Act definitions of "advice" and "intermediary services" and the exclusions (e.g. acts that don't require judgment / don't lead a client to a specific transaction in response to general enquiries).
3. Check any FSCA guidance, conduct standards or commentary on lead generation, referral fees and introducers (CDH, Moonstone, FAnews have written on this case — use them).
4. Check POPIA requirements for consent, purpose specification, direct marketing (s69), retention, data subject rights, and responsible party / operator relationships.
5. Check ECT Act requirements so the agreement can be signed electronically.
Produce a short research memo (research-memo.md) listing every authority relied on, with links, and how each shaped the draft.

DEFINITION OF A QUALIFIED LEAD (heavy-lifting clause)
A Qualified Lead is a South African consumer who:
(a) responded to a Lead Velocity / SortMyCover campaign and gave valid contact details;
(b) gave POPIA-compliant consent to be contacted and introduced to the Client;
(c) self-reported falling within the target criteria: age 35–50 and able to budget R750–R1,250 per month for life cover (criteria are self-declared by the consumer; Lead Velocity does not verify income or underwrite);
(d) confirmed a booked appointment with the Client AND confirmed they will attend.
A lead is delivered and counts toward the 20 at the moment (d) is met. Lead Velocity then sends nurture reminders up to 10 minutes before the appointment. What happens during and after the appointment is solely the Client's responsibility.
Make clear Lead Velocity does not guarantee attendance, suitability, sales or policy outcomes.

NO-SHOW REPLACEMENTS (goodwill, not entitlement)
