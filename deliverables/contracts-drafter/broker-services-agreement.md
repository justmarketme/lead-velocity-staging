Lead Velocity runs the consumer brand **SortMyCover** (sortmycover.co.za). Consumers see SortMyCover in ads and on pages. They meet you by name in WhatsApp.

## 2. Words we use

- **Cycle**: one 30-day period of lead delivery that you have paid for.
- **Committed number**: the number of qualified leads we commit to deliver in a cycle. It is in Schedule A.
- **Qualified lead**: a person who meets every point in Schedule B.
- **Verified**: the person replied, or tapped a button, on WhatsApp within 72 hours of our first message. This proves the number is theirs.
- **Delivered lead**: a qualified lead that is verified and whose details we have given to you.
- **Replacement**: an extra qualified lead we deliver because an earlier one met a rule in Schedule C.
- **Message log**: our time-stamped record of every WhatsApp message, delivery receipt, tap and reply for a lead.
- **Portal**: your login at app.leadvelocity.co.za, where you see your leads, briefs, outcomes and reports.
- **Price**: the flat price for one cycle, in Schedule A.

## 3. What we do for you

3.1 We find people who want to talk about life cover. We run the ads, pages and WhatsApp chat. We pay for the ads from our own ad account.

3.2 We check each person against Schedule B. We tell each person, in their first WhatsApp, that their details have gone to you. That message names you, your practice and your FSP number.

3.3 **Booking is a service we perform for you. It is not what you buy.** We offer the person times from your calendar, book the call, send reminders and help them reschedule. We send you a short brief before each call.

3.4 We send you a report every week and a summary at the end of each cycle.

3.5 We send each lead only to you. We never sell or give the same lead to anyone else.

## 4. What you pay, and how

4.1 **One payment buys one cycle.** You pay the price in Schedule A before the cycle starts. The price is a flat amount in rand, excluding VAT. VAT is added only once we are VAT-registered.

4.2 **The price never depends on policies.** It is the same whether you sell no policies or many. We never charge per policy, a share of premium, or a share of commission. We never ask for payment when a sale happens. You may choose to tell us how many policies you wrote, so we can show you your return. We never use that number to set or change any fee.

4.3 **When a cycle starts.** Your first cycle starts on the day we switch your lead routing on after your payment clears. Each later cycle starts the day after the one before ends, if you have paid for it.

4.4 **How to pay.** You can pay by:
- Instant EFT through our payment page (the default);
- manual EFT to our bank account, using the reference on your invoice (no fees); or
- card, with automatic renewal **only if you switch it on**. You can switch it off in the portal at any time, and the next cycle will not be charged.

4.5 **No contract term. No notice period. No grace period.** You buy one cycle at a time. There is no minimum number of cycles. You do not have to tell us you are stopping. If you do not pay for the next cycle by the time the current one ends, new leads simply stop at the end of that cycle. Nothing more is owed. Leads already delivered stay yours.

4.6 **Next cycle.** Seven days before your cycle ends, we send you a results summary and a payment link for the next cycle. You may change tier for the next cycle. Changes take effect at the start of a cycle.

## 5. Delivery, shortfall and replacements

5.1 We aim to deliver the committed number within the cycle. A lead counts toward the committed number only once it is **verified** and meets Schedule B.

5.2 **If we fall short.** If we have not delivered the committed number by the end of the cycle, the cycle carries on for up to 14 more days, at no cost, until we do. If we are still short after those 14 days, we credit you for each lead short. The credit is the price divided by the committed number, for each missing lead. The credit comes off your next cycle. If you do not buy a next cycle, we refund it to you within 10 business days.

5.3 **Replacements.** Some leads can be replaced. The rules are in Schedule C. A replaced lead stops counting toward your committed number, so we deliver another. Replacements are limited to the cap in Schedule A for each cycle.

5.4 We do not replace a lead because the person did not buy, or because you did not like the outcome of the call. That is never a reason.

5.5 If a shortfall extension overlaps a new paid cycle, leads count first toward the older cycle. The new cycle then starts when the older one has its committed number or its 14 days run out.

## 6. We do not give advice. You are the FSP.

6.1 We are not a financial services provider. We do not give advice. We do not compare products. We do not quote premiums or cover amounts. We do not sell, or help sell, any financial product. Our ads, pages and messages only explain why a call with a licensed adviser may help, and book that call.

6.2 You are the only financial services provider in this arrangement. All FAIS duties toward the person are yours. This includes your disclosures, needs analysis, advice, record of advice, product choice and complaints process.

6.3 Our WhatsApp assistant answers questions about the call only. If a person asks about products, cover or premiums, it tells them you will answer on the call. It never says anything that could be advice.

6.4 Lead Velocity is not your representative and does not act for any insurer. We do not collect premiums, sign up clients, or handle policy paperwork.

## 7. What you promise us

7.1 You promise that, for as long as this agreement runs:

- the adviser who takes the calls is properly authorised and not debarred;

7.2 You must tell us within one business day if any of this stops being true. We will then pause your routing until it is fixed.

## 8. Leads are yours. Routing and exclusivity.

8.1 Each delivered lead is **yours to use exclusively**. We never give, sell or show it to another broker.

8.2 We may work with other brokers. We share leads between brokers by fixed rules: capacity, any area or language you agreed in Schedule A, and otherwise in turn. You do not get sole rights to an area unless Schedule A says so.

8.3 If the same person makes a new enquiry more than 90 days later, it is a new enquiry with new consent. It may go to any broker under these rules.

## 9. Personal information (POPIA)

9.1 **Our role.** We are the responsible party for collecting each person's details and consent, and for our own messages to them. We keep the consent record, the message log and the suppression list.

9.2 **Your role.** Once we hand a lead to you, you are a responsible party for what you do with it. You must protect it as POPIA requires.

9.3 **Where we act for you.** When we put a booking in your calendar or send an invite for you, we do it on your instructions. For that work we are your **operator**. For that work we will:
- use the details only to book the call;
- keep them safe;
- tell you within 24 hours if we find a security breach; and
- use only service providers who keep the same standard.

9.4 **What you may use a lead for.** Only to contact that person about their life cover enquiry, and to give them financial services they ask for. You may not sell, share, rent or pool leads. You may not add them to a marketing list for other products, unless the person agrees to it directly with you.

9.5 **Opt-outs.** If a person replies STOP, objects, or is on the national opt-out registry, we tell you within 24 hours through the portal. You must stop marketing to them. We clean our records against the National Consumer Commission opt-out registry every month. We keep one suppression list. A registered block wins over any earlier consent. If you market to leads yourself, you must follow the same rules.

9.6 **Deleting.** If a person asks us to delete their details, we delete ours and tell you. You must then delete yours within 30 days, unless a law makes you keep them, such as FAIS record-keeping. In that case keep only what the law requires.

9.7 **How long we keep data.** We keep lead details for 12 months after our last contact with the person, then delete them. We keep consent records for 5 years as evidence.

9.8 **Security breaches.** Each of us tells the other within 24 hours of finding a breach that affects leads. Each of us then tells the Information Regulator and the people affected as POPIA section 22 requires, for the data it is responsible for.

9.9 **Health and ID details.** If a person tells us health details or an ID number in chat, we remove them from our records. Your brief will only say they have a question for you.

## 11. Your name, face and voice

11.1 **Required.** We must use your name, practice name and FSP number in the first WhatsApp to each lead. This is how the person learns who will contact them. It is part of the service.

11.2 **Your choice.** You may also allow us to use your headshot, bio, intro voice note and intro video in:

- reminders and pre-call messages to those leads; and

People show up more often when they have seen and heard you. But this is optional. If you say no, we send a text-only intro card.

11.3 We will not use your photo, voice or video in public ads unless you agree in writing for a specific ad. We will never clone your voice, make an avatar of you, or change what you said.

11.4 You may withdraw the choice in 11.2 at any time in the portal. We stop using those items for new messages within 2 business days. Messages already sent cannot be recalled. Withdrawing does not change the price or the committed number.

11.5 When we stop working together, we delete your recordings and photo within 30 days. We keep only copies inside message logs, as evidence.

11.6 You may download your intro video and use it yourself.

## 12. Meta fallback: ads from your Page

12.1 Meta may ask for proof of a financial services licence before it runs our ads. If that happens, we may run the same ads from your Facebook Page, paid by our ad account. Annex 1 is your written permission for this. We use it only if Meta requires it.

## 13. Pausing and ending

13.1 **Ending is simple.** Do not pay for the next cycle. Routing stops at the end of the current cycle (and any shortfall extension).

13.2 **We may pause routing** at once if: your FSP licence or the adviser's authority lapses or is suspended; you breach clause 9.4; or you no longer meet Schedule D in a way that harms leads (for example, repeated missed calls). We tell you why in writing. When fixed, we resume and the cycle is extended by the days paused.

13.3 **If we end the agreement during a paid cycle** because of a reason in 13.2, or for our own reasons, we refund the undelivered part of the cycle using the formula in clause 5.2.

13.4 After the end, clauses 8, 9, 10, 11.5, 14 and 15 still apply.

## 14. Limits on liability

14.1 Our total liability to you in any cycle is capped at the price you paid for that cycle.

14.2 We are not liable for: advice you give; policies you sell or do not sell; income you hoped to earn; or indirect loss.

14.3 Nothing here limits liability for fraud, or for gross negligence, where the law does not allow it to be limited.

## 15. Disagreements

15.1 Disputes about a single lead follow Schedule C.

15.2 For anything else, either of us may write to the other. Our two decision-makers will talk within 5 business days.

15.3 If that does not settle it within 10 business days, either of us may refer it to mediation by a mediator we agree on. Each pays its own costs and half of the mediator.

15.4 If mediation fails, either of us may go to a court with jurisdiction in South Africa.

## 16. General

16.1 South African law applies.

16.2 This agreement, its Schedules and Annex 1 are the whole deal. It replaces any earlier agreement or term sheet for the same services.

16.3 Changes must be in writing and accepted by both of us. Accepting in the portal counts as writing.

16.4 We may change prices or terms only for a future cycle. We tell you at least 7 days before that cycle, in the renewal message. You decide whether to buy it.

16.5 Signing electronically in the portal is valid. The portal records who signed, when, and from which device.

16.6 Notices go to the email addresses in clause 1.

16.7 Neither of us may transfer this agreement without the other's written consent.

## Schedule B — What counts as a qualified lead

A qualified lead is a person who meets **all** of these:

1. **Age 35 to 50**, as they told us. We ask using bands: under 35 / 35–44 / 45–50 / 51 and over. Only 35–44 and 45–50 qualify.
2. **Monthly budget band** of R750–R1,250 **or** R1,250 and more. Both bands qualify. We ask with a multiple-choice band, never exact income.
3. **A valid South African mobile number** (not a landline or internet number), **POPIA consent recorded**, and **verified**: they replied or tapped on WhatsApp within 72 hours of our first message.
4. **Agrees to a video, WhatsApp or phone call** with an adviser.
5. **Not a duplicate**: not already sent to any broker in the last 90 days.

What a person says about age and budget is their own statement. You will check their real needs and affordability on the call, as your FAIS duties require.

## Schedule C — Replacements and disputes

**C1. When a lead can be replaced**

| Trigger | What it means | How it is decided | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| **No-show** | The person did not attend **and** did not answer our check 30 minutes after the call ("Did {adviser} reach you?") | You mark `No-show`; our check gets no answer. We offer the person new times. If they have not rebooked within 48 hours, or they miss a second time, the replacement is due. | outcome `No-show` |
| **Uncontactable** | We cannot reach a verified lead | Decided by the system, not by opinion: our message is not delivered, **or** there is no reply through our full follow-up sequence, **or** you mark `Unreachable / wrong number` and our log agrees | `unreachable` |
| **Disqualified** | The person does not meet a point in Schedule B | You mark `Not a fit – outside criteria` and choose a reason: age band, budget band, not their number, would not take a call, or duplicate | `nofit_criteria` + reason code |

**C1A. When the person cancels** *(DRAFT — default pending Jonathan's choice, NH-42. Options (a) and (b) are in the drafting note below.)*

If a verified lead cancels the call, one of three things happens:

1. **They book again.** The new call is judged like any other call. Nothing is replaced yet.
2. **They say plainly that they do not want a call** (for example "I don't want a call" or "No thanks"). We stop messaging them at once. The lead counts as **Disqualified**, with the reason "would not take a call", and can be replaced. Code: `nofit_criteria`, reason `no_call`.
3. **They go quiet.** We offer new times once. Then we send our full follow-up sequence. If they have not booked again by the end of it, the lead counts as **Uncontactable** and can be replaced. Code: `unreachable`, reason `cancel_no_rebook`.

The system decides points 2 and 3 from the message log. These replacements count against your cap for the cycle. The 48-hour dispute rule in C3 applies as usual.

> *Drafting note — the two options for C1A (Jonathan picks one; the default above is (a) + (b)):*
>
> **Option (a) only — "Cancelled and did not rebook" counts as Uncontactable.** If a verified lead cancels the call, we offer new times once. Then we send our full follow-up sequence. If the person has not booked again by the end of it, the lead counts as **Uncontactable** and can be replaced. The system decides this from the message log. Code: `unreachable`, reason `cancel_no_rebook`. If the person says plainly that they do not want a call, we stop messaging them at once, and that lead is not replaced under this option.
>

The lead counts as **Disqualified**, with the reason "would not take a call", and can be replaced. We stop messaging the person at once. The message log, or your mark with that reason, is the evidence. Code: `nofit_criteria`, reason `no_call`. A lead who cancels without saying this, and simply does not book again, is not replaced under this option.

**C2. What is never replaced**

| Situation | Code | Why |

| **You** missed the call | — | See Schedule D. We apologise to the person and rebook at our cost. No replacement. |
| Call not marked within 24 hours | — | Recorded as attended (Schedule D) |

**C3. How a claim works**

1. A claim opens automatically when an outcome or code in C1 is recorded. You do not need to write to us.
2. **We have 48 hours to dispute it.** We may only dispute using the message log (for example, it shows the person answered our check and said you did not call).
3. If we do not dispute within 48 hours, the replacement is approved.
4. If we dispute, we show you the log in the portal. You may reply with your own evidence within 48 hours. Our decision-maker then decides within 2 business days and writes down why. If you still disagree, clause 15 applies.
5. Approved replacements count against your cap for that cycle. Once the cap is reached, no more replacements are due in that cycle.
6. Replacements are delivered in the same cycle or its extension. Any not delivered are treated as a shortfall under clause 5.2.

## Schedule D — Your service levels

1. **Mark every call within 24 hours.** Use the WhatsApp buttons or the portal: outcome, then code, then a 1–5 quality score. If you do not mark it within 24 hours, it is recorded as `attended`.
2. **Attend every booked call** at the booked time, by the booked method. If you miss a call, we apologise to the person and offer a new time at our cost. You do not get a replacement for that lead.
3. **Keep your calendar accurate.** Block time you are not available. We book only into free time we can see.
4. **Give your own FAIS disclosures** on every call, as the FAIS General Code of Conduct requires. Our WhatsApp disclosure does not replace yours.
5. **Use leads only yourself** (clause 9.4). Never resell or share them.
6. **Act on opt-outs** within 24 hours of our notice, and delete on request (clause 9.6).
7. **Keep your FSP details current** and tell us of any change within one business day.
