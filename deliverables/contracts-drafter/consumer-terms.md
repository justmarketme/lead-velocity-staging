**Terms of use**
Last updated: {{date}} · Version TU-v1.0

These terms explain what SortMyCover does, what it does not do, and what we ask of you. Please read them with our privacy notice. By using this site or our WhatsApp, you agree to these terms. **(practitioner — Q28)**

### 1. Who we are
SortMyCover is a service of Lead Velocity (Pty) Ltd. We connect you with authorised financial services providers. We do not give financial advice, compare products or quote premiums.

Lead Velocity (Pty) Ltd, registration number {{CIPC_REG_NO}}, {{ADDRESS_STREET}}, {{ADDRESS_CITY}}, {{ADDRESS_POSTCODE}}. We are not a financial services provider. **(practitioner — Q27: is this enough company information for ECTA s43?)**

### 2. What we do
- We ask you a few short questions. They check that a call with an adviser suits you.
- If it suits you, and you agree, we pass your details to one adviser. The adviser is named when you agree, or in our first WhatsApp to you. That message shows their name, practice and FSP number.
- We book your call and send you reminders on WhatsApp.

Your free cover check is a conversation with that adviser. It is a call of about 30 minutes.

### 3. What we do not do
- We do not give financial advice.
- We do not compare products, quote premiums or sell policies.
- Our WhatsApp assistant uses AI. It can answer questions about the call and help you book. It never gives advice. Type "person" at any time to reach a human.
- What you read on this site is general information. It is not advice. Only a licensed adviser can tell you what fits you.

### 4. It is free, and you do not have to buy
You pay us nothing. You do not have to buy anything, on the call or after it.

Advisers pay us a flat fee for each 30-day cycle. The fee is the same whether or not you buy. We earn nothing from any policy. See [How we make money](/how-we-make-money.html).

### 5. Your adviser
Your adviser is an authorised financial services provider. They run their own business. They are not our employee or agent. **(practitioner — Q29)** They are responsible for any advice they give you and any product they offer. They must give you their own disclosures on the call.

### 6. You can stop at any time
- Reply **STOP** to any WhatsApp, or email howzit@leadvelocity.co.za with the word STOP. We stop at once and tell the adviser.
- You can cancel or move your call at any time. Use the buttons in our WhatsApp, or reply to us.
- You can also join the National Consumer Commission opt-out registry. We check our records against it every month. A registered block always wins, even if you agreed before.

### 7. Your details
Our [privacy notice](/privacy.html) explains how we collect, use, share and keep your details, and your rights.

### 8. What we ask of you
- You must be 18 or older.
- Give true details, and only your own number. Do not enter someone else's details.
- Do not misuse this site or our WhatsApp. For example, do not send spam or harmful files, or try to get into our systems.

If you do not, we may close your enquiry and not pass on your details.

### 9. Information and links
We try to keep this site correct and up to date. Examples on this site are general. They are not quotes, and they do not promise any result. We are not responsible for other websites we link to.

### 10. Our responsibility
We are responsible for running our own service with care. We are not responsible for advice an adviser gives you, or for a product you choose. **(practitioner — Q26)** Nothing in these terms takes away your rights under the Consumer Protection Act or the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). **(practitioner — Q25)**

### 11. Complaints and contact
Email howzit@leadvelocity.co.za, or send **COMPLAINT** on our WhatsApp. We reply within **48 hours**. See also [complaints](/complaints.html).

Complaints about an adviser's advice go to the adviser's own complaints process. If it is not solved, you can go to the FAIS Ombud.

### 12. Law and changes

| Q25 | The consumer pays nothing. Does the CPA apply to SortMyCover's free introduction and booking service, and should the terms name it? | Name the CPA in a saving line only ("nothing takes away your rights"), and write the whole page to the s22 plain-language standard as if it applies | Drop the CPA name and keep a general saving line ("any rights the law gives you") |
| Q26 | Is the one-line limit in §10 ("not responsible for advice an adviser gives you, or for a product you choose") acceptable under CPA s48–s51, and does s49 need it to be shown more prominently? | One plain line, no money cap, no exclusion for our own negligence, followed by the CPA/POPIA saving line | (a) Remove the line and rely on §3 and §5; or (b) keep it and make it prominent (bold box) under s49 |
| Q27 | Does ECTA s43 apply to this site (the consumer buys nothing online)? If yes, what must we add to §1? | Give the core identity facts on the page (full name, legal status, registration number, physical address, email; phone via the IO block) | Add the full s43(1) list (for example, office bearers, place of registration, address for legal service) in a short "Company information" block |
| Q28 | Are the terms binding through a footer link and the line "By using this site or our WhatsApp, you agree", or must the consent area also link to them? | Footer link on every page plus the opening line. The consent tick links the privacy notice only, so the versioned consent text (`CONSENT-NAMED-v1`) does not change | Add "and Terms" next to the tick. ## Reading-level check (hand-checked, no tool run)

- Same discipline as the privacy page: one idea per sentence; most sentences 6 to 15 words; no "hereby", "notwithstanding", "indemnify", "shall", "herein" or "party/parties" in the published text.
- Plain words chosen: "stop" not "withdraw consent", "pass your details to" not "disclose", "not responsible for" not "not liable for".
- Defined terms the reader may not know are explained where they first appear (FSP number shown in the first WhatsApp; "authorised financial services provider" used as on every other page).
- Longest sentence: §6 opt-out line (about 20 words), copied from the privacy page to keep the two pages identical.
- Banned words checked: no "guaranteed", "best", "cheapest", "save", no outcome claims. "Free cover check" is described as a conversation, not a result.
- Length: about 780 words of published text, shorter than the privacy page.
- A machine Flesch-Kincaid run is still owed (no shell available to this agent). Estimate: Grade 6 to 7.
