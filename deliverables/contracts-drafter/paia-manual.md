## 8. How to request access

1. Complete the prescribed request form for a private body ({{paia_request_form_number — confirm, currently "Form 2"}}).
2. Send it to the Information Officer at howzit@leadvelocity.co.za or the postal address above.
3. Give enough detail for us to find the record, say in what form you want it, and give your contact details.
4. If you ask on behalf of someone else, attach proof of your authority.
5. If you need the record to protect or exercise a right, say which right and why.
6. Pay the request fee (section 9). We tell you if a deposit or access fee applies.
7. We decide within **30 days**. We may extend once by up to 30 days, and will tell you why.
8. If we refuse, we give reasons. You can complain to the Regulator or apply to court.

We may refuse access on the grounds in Chapter 4 of Part 3 of PAIA (for example, to protect another person's privacy or commercial information).

## 10. Processing of personal information (POPIA section 51 and section 18)

### 10.1 Purposes
- Connecting consumers who ask for it with one authorised financial services provider about insurance and financial planning
- Booking and reminding consumers of calls; sending invites
- Measuring and improving advertising (hashed data only)
- Running broker accounts, billing and reporting
- Keeping consent, opt-out and compliance records
- Meeting legal duties (tax, company law, POPIA, CPA)

### 10.2 Data subjects and categories of information

| Data subject | Information |
|---|---|
| Consumers (leads) | First name and surname, mobile, optional email (invite only), optional call and backup numbers, age band, budget band, reason(s) for the call, bond/dependants/cover through work, optional spend and income bands, optional smoker status, preferred call method, booking times, messages, consent record, ad source, device and IP data |
| Brokers and advisers | Names, practice, FSP number, contact details, calendar availability, headshot, bio, voice and video recordings (with consent), payment records, feedback |
| Suppliers | Contact and banking details, contracts |
| Staff and contractors | Contact, identity, tax and payment details |

Smoker status (special personal information, POPIA s26) is asked only as an optional question with the consumer's express consent (s27(1)(a)) and is used only to brief the adviser named in the question. No other special personal information is asked for; if volunteered, it is removed from stored records.

### 10.3 Recipients
- The one authorised FSP a consumer agreed to be passed to
- Our operators: Meta, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Twilio, Paystack, Hostinger, Supabase
- Regulators and authorities where the law requires

### 10.4 Cross-border flows
Some operators process data outside South Africa. We use them only where POPIA section 72 allows: the recipient is bound by law or binding agreement giving protection like POPIA, or the transfer is needed for the service the person asked for. See the Privacy Notice for the list.

### 10.5 Security measures (summary)
- Access by role; brokers see only their own leads
- Secrets kept out of code; encrypted connections (TLS)
- Health and ID details redacted from transcripts; logs mask numbers and emails
- Nightly backups copied off the server
- Monthly cleanse against the NCC opt-out registry; single suppression list
- Retention: lead data deleted 12 months after last contact; consent records kept 5 years
- Breach procedure under POPIA section 22
