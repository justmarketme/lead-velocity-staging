## No email to Meta (review 4 #27, 2026-10-02)
Email no longer goes to Meta by any path. `capi.js` drops the `em` user-data field and ignores any email it is given (even a pre-hashed `em`), and the customer-list audience schema no longer has an `EMAIL` column; `meta-ads.js` rejects any audience schema that includes one (`EMAIL_NOT_ALLOWED`). This follows the 0.1 Email row: email is collected only for a Teams, Zoom or Meet invite and used only for that. The hashed phone number (`ph`), `fn`, `external_id` (SHA-256 of `lead_id`), `fbp`/`fbc`, server-side IP and user agent, and `ctwa_clid` still go out. `event-spec.md` now expects a lower EMQ and lists what lifts it without email; `privacy.html` says the number is hashed and "We never send your email to Meta." No other builder in automation/ sends `em`. Tests: CAPI 12/12 (2 added), meta-ads 22/22 (1 added).
