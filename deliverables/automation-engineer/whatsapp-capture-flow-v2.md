# WhatsApp capture flow v2: spec and exact screen copy

Owner: automation-engineer (Head of Automation) · Decided by Jonathan, 2026-10-07 · Status: built and tested offline, not live.
Code: `automation/ctwa/capture-v2.js` (all copy below comes from its `COPY` object; a test fails if this file drifts from it) · Flow JSON: `automation/flows/capture-flow-v2.json` (generated by `build-capture-flow-v2.mjs`) · Tests: `automation/tests/capture-v2.test.mjs` · Migration (file only, not applied): `supabase/migrations/20261007190000_smc_19_capture_v2.sql`.

## The path in one line
Ad (click-to-WhatsApp, or the landing page) → **consent tap** (W03, wording `ctwa-named-v3`, v3 scope) → WhatsApp number captured and marked **verified** (the inbound message proves it: `wa_id`, `wa_verified = true`) → **capture Flow** (screens inside WhatsApp, no browser) → tier A books a Teams call in the Flow and gets the broker welcome card; tier B is offered to the broker first; anyone outside the bands is closed politely and never delivered or counted.

## Screen by screen (exact copy)
Every screen posts to our endpoint (`data_exchange`), which checks the answer and picks the next screen. Answers are saved after every screen, so a lead who stops halfway is still on record.

**0. Consent (W03, buttons, `ctwa-named-v3`).** "Before we start: if it's a fit, we'll share your details with {practice} (FSP {fsp}), an authorised financial services provider who'll contact you about insurance and financial planning. OK to continue?" plus the responsible-party line (v3 scope, same as `CONSENT-NAMED-v3` on the landing page). Buttons `Yes, continue` · `No thanks`. Then the Flow opens with this message:
> Thanks {first name}. A few quick taps and you can pick a time with an adviser.

Button: `Start`

**1. What can we help with?** (required, multi-select)
> What would you like to talk to an adviser about?

Choose all that apply: `Life cover` · `Funeral cover` · `Retirement planning` · `Investments` · `Disability cover` · `I feel like I'm paying too much` · `Something else`
Error: "Please choose at least one."

**2. Your current cover** (optional for everyone; comes right after screen 1)
If "I feel like I'm paying too much" was ticked:
> You said you might be paying too much. What are you paying at the moment? (Optional)

Otherwise:
> What are you paying at the moment? (Optional)

Total per month: `Under R500` · `R500 to R1,000` · `R1,000 to R1,500` · `R1,500 to R2,500` · `R2,500 or more` · `Prefer not to say`

**3. Your name** (both required)
> What is your name?

Fields: `Name` · `Surname`. Error: "Please enter your real name and surname, letters only."

**4. Your email** (email required; alternative optional)
> Where should we send your meeting invite?

Fields: `Email` · tick box `Same as above (no other email)` · `Alternative email (optional)`.
Checks while they wait (< 300 ms): format, typo hint ("Did you mean lerato@gmail.com?", pre-filled, not counted as a failed try), disposable domains, MX record. Error: "That email address doesn’t look right. Please check it."

**5. Your number**
> Is 082 555 0101 the number to call you on?

(Template: `Is {wa_number} the number to call you on?`.) Options: `Yes, use my WhatsApp number` · `No, use another number` · field `Other number (only if you chose another)`.
Same → `call_number` = the WhatsApp number, verified. Another → SA mobile format + Twilio Lookup line type (landline/VoIP rejected), stored as validated but not verified. Errors: "Please type the other number, or choose your WhatsApp number." / "Please use a South African mobile number."

**6. Your age** (required)
> Which age group are you in?

`Under 35` · `35 to 44` · `45 to 50` · `51 or older`. Bands per 0.1. Under 35 and 51+ are out of band (same rule as W01/W03) → polite close.

**7. Your budget** (required)
> Roughly what could you put towards cover each month?

`R1,500 or more` · `R1,250 to R1,499` · `R750 to R1,249` · `Under R750`
- R1,500 or more → **tier A**: delivered and counted automatically.
- R750 to R1,499 → **tier B**: offered to the broker first (see below).
- Under R750 → polite close; never delivered, never counted, deleted within 24 h (existing out-of-band handling).

**8. One more thing** (optional)
> Do you smoke? (Optional — used only to brief your adviser {broker_first_name} before the call.)

As shown: "Do you smoke? (Optional — used only to brief your adviser Mark before the call.)" Options: `No` · `Yes` · `Prefer not to say`.
Stored with the exact wording shown and the time answered (`smoker_question_text`, `smoker_answered_at`) as consent evidence. **Never sent to Meta** (CAPI, Pixel, custom data). Enforced by `capiCustomData` and tested.

**9. Your income** (optional)
> What is your monthly income before tax? (Optional)

`Under R20,000` · `R20,000 to R39,999` · `R40,000 to R59,999` · `R60,000 to R99,999` · `R100,000 or more` · `Prefer not to say`. Never sent to Meta.

**10. Pick a day** → **Pick a time** → **Check and book** (tier A only)
> Pick a day for your Teams call with {adviser}

WhatsApp's own calendar (CalendarPicker, field label "Day"): earliest = now + the broker's notice, latest = +14 days, only his working days, full/blocked/holiday days greyed out (W04 rules over his Outlook free/busy). Picking a day shows that day's free times. Summary example: "Thu 8 Oct at 10:00 (South African time), 30-minute Microsoft Teams call with Mark from Mark Smith Financial Services (FSP 12345). We’ll email the Teams link to lerato@example.co.za." Button: `Book my call`. Other footer buttons: `Continue` · `Done`.
Booking re-checks free/busy at the moment of the tap (W05: zero double-bookings). Error: "That day just filled up. Please pick another day."

**11. Welcome card + end screen**
> You're booked
> {adviser} from {practice} (FSP {fsp}) will meet you on Teams on {when}. We have emailed the Teams invite to {email}. Your confirmation is on its way here on WhatsApp.

Then the existing `broker_intro_booked` card (intro image, practice, FSP number, adviser, Teams, date, time, STOP line) arrives in the chat. That is the disclosure message; its message id and delivery are logged as evidence.

### Other endings (same END screen)
- Tier B: **We're matching you with an adviser**: "Thanks {first_name}. We're matching you with an authorised adviser now. We'll message you here on WhatsApp shortly so you can pick a time."
- Out of band: **Thank you**: "Thanks for your time, {first_name}. Based on your answers, the advisers we work with aren't the right fit right now, so we won't pass your details on. Take care."
- Invalid contact details (3rd bad name, email or number): "Thanks for your time. We couldn't confirm your contact details, so we can't pass your enquiry on right now. You can message us again at any time."
- No free times / calendar down / broker has no Teams: **Almost done**: "Thanks {first_name}. We'll send you {adviser}'s next free times here on WhatsApp in a moment." (we send the 10-slot list on WhatsApp).
- Time taken at the last second: "That time was just taken. We'll send you the next free times here on WhatsApp in a moment."

## Tier B: broker offer (R750 to R1,499)
1. The Flow ends on the "matching" screen, and the lead gets on WhatsApp:
> Thanks {first_name}. We're matching you with an authorised adviser now. We'll message you here shortly so you can pick a time.

2. The broker gets template `broker_lead_offer` (utility) and the same Accept / Decline on the portal lead row:
> Hi {{1}}, a new SortMyCover enquiry is available to you: {{2}}, age {{3}}, budget {{4}} a month, interested in {{5}}. This lead is below R1,500 a month, so it only counts if you accept it. Please accept or decline by {{6}}. If you don't reply by then, it goes to another adviser.

Buttons: `Accept` · `Decline`

3. **Accept** → delivered and counted toward his committed number; the lead gets the existing `broker_intro_slots_v2` card (disclosure + "Pick a time" Flow, books Teams via W28/W05). Broker sees: "Accepted. {first_name} is now your lead and gets your booking card."
4. **Decline** → not delivered to him, not counted; broker sees "Declined. {first_name} will not be sent to you and does not count." The lead goes to the next eligible broker, never back to one who declined or let it lapse. In named-consent mode the lead first gets a fresh consent tap naming the new practice (the original consent named only the first one). No eligible broker → held, and the lead gets:
> Thanks for your patience, {first_name}. All our advisers are fully booked right now. We'll message you here as soon as one is free.

5. **Expiry (my choice):** 2 *working* hours (Mon–Fri 08:00–17:00 SAST, SA public holidays skipped; an offer made after hours starts its clock at the next opening). Expired = declined, recorded as `expired`.
6. Evidence: a `lead_offers` row per broker (offered/decided/expiry timestamps, how decided) + `offer_status` / `offer_decided_at` on the lead. Decisions are idempotent: a double tap or a tap after expiry changes nothing.

## Email verification
After the Flow completes we send one email from howzit@ (Microsoft Graph, the existing mail path) with a one-tap link and a 6-digit code (they can type it on WhatsApp):
> Subject: Confirm your email for your call with {adviser}

> Hi {first_name},
> 
> Please confirm this is your email address so {adviser} can send you your meeting details:
> 
> {link}
> 
> Or reply on WhatsApp with this code: {code}
> 
> The link and code work for 24 hours. If you did not ask for this, ignore this email.
> 
> SortMyCover

Only hashes are stored. The link is tied to the current address (changing the email kills old links); 24-h expiry; 5 wrong codes lock it. Still unverified 24 h after sending → `email_unverified_flag`. The lead stays valid, because the WhatsApp number is verified.

## Licence check
Reasons map to licence categories: life cover and disability → `lt_risk`; funeral → `lt_funeral`; retirement → `retirement`; investments → `investments`; "paying too much" and "something else" → none. If `brokers.licence_categories` is set and a reason isn't covered → `licence_flag = outside_licence`. If it isn't set yet → `licence_check_needed`. The lead is flagged, not blocked.

## What lands in the CRM (leads row)
Tier, reasons, spend band, first name + surname, email (+ verified flag), alternative email, WhatsApp id (verified), call number (+ verified flag, line type), age band, budget band, smoker + exact wording + time, income band, licence flag, campaign / ad set / ad ids + UTMs, broker_id, offer status and timestamps.

## Dispute evidence (admin)
`disputeEvidence()` builds one pack per lead: consent text/version/time/source, WhatsApp verification, disclosure message id + delivery, every answer with timestamps (including the smoker wording), offers and decisions, the booking (Graph event id, Teams link present, invite status), email verification, flags and a timeline. It lists any gaps (`consent_missing`, `disclosure_missing`, `disclosure_not_delivered`, `wa_not_verified`) and never includes codes or tokens.
