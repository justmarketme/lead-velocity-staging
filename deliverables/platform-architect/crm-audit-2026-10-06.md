# Lead Velocity CRM / SortMyCover audit: chat → CRM → broker allocation

Date: 2026-10-06. Repo: `C:\Users\Jono\lead-velocity-sortmycover`, branch `claude/relaxed-edison-0hcidb`. The audit was taken at c012e81 and re-verified at HEAD 0bb9e37/5bc5df3. No audited files changed between those commits.
Method: six read-only subsystem audits, each followed by a verification pass and two research passes. Every status below is the **post-verification** status.

## 1. Executive summary

**Goal (Jonathan):** a visitor chats on sortmycover.co.za. The conversation lands in the CRM tagged with campaign, ad set, ad, angle and UTM. The lead goes to one broker, chosen next-in-line against that broker's committed number for the cycle, with admin control (pause, caps, weights, reassign). Finishing broker onboarding switches allocation on automatically.

**What works (offline tests, real code)**
- The Thandi conversation core (`conversation/logic.mjs`, `guardrail.mjs`). FAIS prefilter, output gate, STOP, redaction and tone all pass at 100% in the dry-run eval: 238 golden turns, 126 red-team cases, 0% false positives on 217 harmless cases.
- Single-broker routing at page intake (W01 `decide` → `pickBroker`). Exclusivity holds because routing only writes `WHERE broker_id IS NULL`. Real n8n plus Postgres rehearsal in Round 5.
- Data model design: `leads` has attribution, consent, routing and stage columns. `cycles`/`pricing` snapshot a flat fee per 30-day cycle (Bronze 20/4, Silver 30/6, Gold 45/9). `v_cycle_progress` and replacement caps per cycle (W13, 18/18 tests) are in place.
- Live holding site: strict CSP, no scripts, no forms.
- Offline test suites: 765/771 pass, 6 skipped, 0 fail. SMC typecheck is clean and the build succeeds.

**What doesn't work**
1. **Nothing is live.** All 17 `smc_*` migrations are file-only. The live Supabase project (`cmsylaupctrbsvzrgzwy`) has none of them. It also has about 25 migrations and 7 tables that are not in the repo, so there is unresolved drift (NH-11). No workflow is imported into n8n, and all are `active:false`.
2. **Attribution is lost on page leads.** `pixel.js` sends `context.utm.utm_source`, but `w01.normaliseSubmission:133` reads `utm.source`, so every UTM is NULL. `cid/asid/adid` are captured by nothing. Tests pass only because the fixtures use a flat shape the page never sends. W02 drops angle and concept. Campaign and ad set on CTWA leads are never back-filled.
3. **Multi-broker allocation is broken.**
   - The CTWA, held-lead and lead-ad hand-over loader omits the counters, so every lead goes to the lowest broker id.
   - There is no cap at the committed number (a broker at 25/20 still gets leads).
   - `routing_rules`, capacity and weights are ignored.
   - W03 and W01 pick brokers by different rules, and named consent then holds the lead.
   - Held leads are never re-driven and nobody is alerted.
   - **Additional defect found in verification:** the routeExisting write path reuses idempotency key `w01:routed:<id>` for held and routed rows. A held lead that is later routed is assigned to the broker but gets **no first-touch WhatsApp and no CAPI Lead**, and is stuck.
4. **Onboarding never reaches routing.**
   - Portal events never reach W20: there is no relay, and W20 needs an HMAC signature.
   - The `agreement` and `card` steps are never written, so no broker becomes `onboarded`.
   - W16 calls W20 through Execute Workflow, but W20 has no trigger for it.
   - The pre-flight runner and the `GO_LIVE_HANDOFF_URL` receiver do not exist.
   - No console button exists for "Approve & go live".
   - Cycle 1 is never activated, so `current_cycle_id` stays NULL. Leads then carry `cycle_id` NULL and are invisible in `v_cycle_progress`. That broker also wins every balanced pick.
5. **No admin surface.**
   - `/console` has only Today, Ads, Ask, Payments and Brands.
   - There is no leads list showing campaign or broker, no conversation view, and no broker, allocation or held-lead screen.
   - Handoff alerts deep-link to a `/console/conversations/:id` route that returns 404.
   - Legacy `LeadsTable` crashes when searching SMC leads that have a null email.
   - The legacy send box can message SMC leads with no suppression check and no FAIS gate.

**What's missing for the goal**
- A web chat channel: no widget, no endpoint, and no `web_chat` value in the `origin`/`channel` CHECKs.
- An allocation engine with caps, pacing and reserve-then-commit for named consent.
- The go-live hand-off that starts the cycle.
- An admin console for leads, conversations, brokers and allocation.
- Chat copy still names Lead Velocity on consumer surfaces.

**Fit with LV's model**
- **Exclusive leads:** one assignment per lead (unique), routing only `WHERE broker_id IS NULL`, and dedupe on a phone hash. Dedupe should go **across brands** too, because today it is scoped per brand.
- **Flat fee per 30-day cycle:** pace to the committed number. Stop at the target, then send surplus to the backfill queue or trim spend. **Never route by close rate or policies written.** Zillow-style performance routing is tied to success fees, which conflicts with the FAIS and Raspberry Academy v Oaksure position (2026) (A).
- **Committed numbers:** target = `committed_leads` + approved open replacements. A lead that fails the 72 h verification frees the slot without using up a replacement.
- **Replacement caps:** 20% per cycle, replacement-only, never for "didn't buy". This sits inside US norms (SmartFinancial 10 days, credits only; "didn't write a policy" is not returnable; A).
- **Evidence caveat:** the speed-to-lead stats are observational (HBR 2011, B; Velocity's 391%, C). Use them for SLAs, not for broker-facing promises.

## 5. Web chat agent: evidence and recommended architecture

**Evidence**
- Chat vs form: one peer-reviewed field experiment (J. Business Research 2025, WhatsApp bot vs landing page: 42 vs 7 and 18 vs 5, B2B Brazil, small numbers, B). One RCT (J. Dev. Econ. 2023, chatbot beat web forms, and people disclosed more sensitive data, B). Vendor "3–4×" claims are C. **Run an A/B test against the quiz, judged on cost per verified lead and show rate.**
- Third-party widgets: Meta removed the Messenger chat plugin on 9 May 2024 (B). Drift was taken offline after an Aug 2025 OAuth breach (A). Both point to building our own.
- Duties:
  - Anthropic's Usage Policy requires AI disclosure at the start of every session (A).
  - Under FAIS s1, advice is not allowed. Factual and procedural information is allowed (A).
  - Moffatt v Air Canada: the company is liable for its bot (A).
  - Red-team research (B): advice is the weakest category for every model, and multi-turn pressure took attack success to 96%.
  - POPIA (A): s18 notice before collection; s69 consent asked once, as a tap; s26 health data redacted; s72 offshore processors disclosed.
  - Anthropic keeps API data for 30 days by default (A).
- UX:
  - Chat opens only when the user starts it (no auto-open). On mobile it is a full-screen sheet.
  - Inputs at 16 px or more. `role="log"` on the transcript, with the response announced on completion. Visible Send and Stop controls (B).

**Recommended architecture (fits the static site, strict CSP, POPIA and FAIS)**
1. **Widget:** a self-hosted vanilla `landing/shared/chat.js` plus classes in the site CSS. No inline style and no `<form>`, so it fits `prepare-holding-deploy.mjs`; alternatively update that guard on purpose. It opens on an inline "Ask us a question" entry and a small launcher that never covers the quiz CTA.
2. **Endpoint:** same-origin `landing/api/chat.ts` (Vercel Function, streaming SSE). `default-src 'self'` already allows it, so **no CSP change is needed**. A Supabase Edge Function is the alternative but needs `connect-src` and CORS changes. The function holds the service-role key and n8n HMAC server-side.
3. **Bot check:** the existing invisible Turnstile is verified once at session start, then our own HMAC session token is used. Vercel WAF rule on `/api/chat`, plus caps per session and per IP on turns and tokens, plus a daily spend cap. When the cap is hit the bot falls back to "leave your number" (OWASP LLM10).
4. **Brain:** reuse `conversation/logic.mjs` `decide()` (code decides; the LLM only labels and phrases), `guardrail.mjs` (prefilter, redaction, `outputGate` with a new `web` surface) and `lines.mjs` fixed lines. Haiku 4.5 handles intent and the classifier gate, and fails closed. Sonnet 5.5 handles escalation, matching W07. The flow is buttons first: disclosure → scope → W03 qualifying states → consent tap → mobile number.
5. **Consent:** the `consent.json` line is shown verbatim, with a privacy link in the header. A broker is reserved before named consent and committed on the tap. There is no data row before consent. A session without consent is deleted within 24 h.
6. **Hand-off:** POST to W01 Lead core with `channel/origin='web_chat'`, the `smc.context()` attribution and the consent certificate. Routing, held-if-wrong-practice, CAPI Lead and W06 first-touch then behave exactly as for page leads. W06 handles WhatsApp verification (3.3).
7. **Storage:** a `web_threads` table modelled on `dm_threads`, linked to `lead_id` at consent. Turns go to `communications` with `channel='web'`, redacted.
8. **Handoff to a human:** "Talk to a person" is visible from the first message. It creates an `escalations` row. A console conversation view and reply box are needed (shared with WhatsApp).
9. **Copy fixes required before launch:**
   - Remove "Lead Velocity" from `lines.mjs`, faq.md, corpus and templates.
   - Update `index.html` "collects no personal details" and "A person reads every message".
   - Add web chat and Messenger to `privacy.html`, and widen the Anthropic row beyond WhatsApp.
10. **Cost:** about $0.003–0.005 per gated Haiku turn, roughly R1 per 12-turn lead, inside the 4.11 budget.
