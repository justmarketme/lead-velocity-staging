- No price language. The drafts' "The call costs you nothing" line is replaced in the article bodies by the fee line ("Advisers pay SortMyCover a fee for the service. The fee does not depend on whether you buy anything."). The site-wide quiet CTA under every article still carries the approved `COST_LINE` from `src/lib/site.ts`; change it there if Jono wants it gone everywhere.

- The source title "All you need to know about the UIF" was shortened to "Vuk'uzenzele guide to the UIF (May 2019)" because the wording scan bans "you need to" anywhere in visible copy.
- Reviewer placeholders are not rendered (the model only shows a reviewer block once a verified, non-broker reviewer exists). Fact-checked line reads "SortMyCover editorial team, 17 October 2026", as on every other guide.
