# SortMyCover website imagery

Every people image on the site was **generated on 16 Oct 2026 in Higgsfield** (Google Nano Banana model, routed by Higgsfield as nano_banana_2) under Jono's Higgsfield Plus account, 2 credits each. No stock photography is used (Jono, 16 Oct 2026). The five Pexels photos used until then were removed.

Rules: images are decorative life scenes, never presented as clients, advisers, testimonials or outcomes; no AI label is shown on the site (Jono's decision); each slot has its own image (tests/imagery.test.ts). The prompt for each slot is the `scene` field in `sites/sortmycover/src/content/imagery.ts` plus the shared style line in `flow-image-brief.md`. Re-import with `node scripts/import-scenes.mjs map.json`.
