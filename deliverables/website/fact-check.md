# Fact check: SortMyCover website research ledger

Synthesis lead: written 10 Oct 2026. Scope: the eight research lenses in `deliverables/website/research/` (top5-sites, seo-google, geo-llm-search, ux-cro, meta-pixel-capi, compliance-sa, subdomain-architecture, motion-library), each claim checked twice by adversarial reviewers. This file is the ledger the build spec (`deliverables/website/site-spec.md`) cites. Nothing was deployed, committed or installed.

## 0. How to read this file

Status words, exactly as the reviewers returned them:
- **CONFIRMED**: the claim holds. Use freely. Where a reviewer added a caveat, it is in the Correction column and still applies.
- **PARTLY**: part of the claim holds, part does not. Use only the corrected form in the Correction column. Wherever a reviewer proved a sub-claim wrong, that sub-claim is listed again in section 2 (traps) so nobody re-uses it.
- **REFUTED**: none of the 96 claims came back wholly refuted. Every wrong statement lives inside a PARTLY row and is collected in section 2.
- **UNVERIFIED**: no claim carried this status at claim level, but many corrections contain unverified sub-points. They are collected in section 3 as hypotheses to test, never as facts.

Counts: 96 claims. 26 CONFIRMED, 70 PARTLY. By lens: top5-sites 0/12, seo-google 8/12, geo-llm-search 0/12, ux-cro 5/12, meta-pixel-capi 5/12, compliance-sa 1/12, subdomain-architecture 2/12, motion-library 5/12 (CONFIRMED/total).

IDs: the research files reuse F1 to F12 in several lenses, so this ledger prefixes them. T = top5-sites, S = seo-google, G = geo-llm-search, U = ux-cro (ux-01 is U1), P = meta-pixel-capi ("pixel"), C = compliance-sa, D = subdomain-architecture ("DNS and domains"), M = motion-library. Example: P3 is meta-pixel-capi F3, the opt-out cookie scope. The compliance lens also has writer rules S1 to S35 inside `compliance-sa.md`; the spec cites those as "rule S13" to avoid clashing with the S-prefixed SEO claims.

Evidence rules held throughout: a source URL with a date wherever the reviewers gave one; "fetched 10 Oct 2026" where a page showed no date; anything older than 2024 is flagged STALE-RISK; no statistic is stated without its source.

Limits every reader should know: SAFLII returned HTTP 403 throughout, so the text of *Raspberry Academy v Oaksure* [2026] ZAGPJHC 388 (14 Apr 2026) was never read directly by any reviewer; its holding comes from Moonstone, CDH, FAnews, ENS and Deneys. The FAIS Act text used is a Sabinet copy printed 25 Jun 2018 and cross-read on SAFLII, acts.co.za and lawlibrary.org.za. Several benchmark sites (Compare the Market, Ethos, Lemonade, Insurify) blocked the fetcher. Nothing here is legal advice; every legal conclusion is for the compliance practitioner.

## 1. What changed our minds (read this first)

Thirty-one corrections move the build. Grouped by what they change.

**Strategy and law**
1. "Cannot copy the leaders' CTA" is too absolute (T1). What is established: elements that recommend, rank, transact or market a specific product carry high FAIS risk for an unlicensed party. Neutral factual material is copyable. A flat-fee, mechanical contact hand-over is lower risk but NOT established as safe: the court only said it "might prompt a different factual enquiry", and no commentator endorses a safe harbour. The spec therefore designs the site so no page recommends, ranks, prices or transacts, and asks the practitioner to review the live site, not only the contract.
2. The closest model (Unbiased) is an appointed representative of an FCA-authorised principal, but that rests on Unbiased's own terms page alone, and its other pages cite different regulatory entities (T2). It is a prompt for question Q1 to the attorney, not a template to copy.
3. SortMyCover already says "free and how we are paid" in more places than the research claimed (T3): every footer, terms s4, about, a learn page, the quiz FAQ. The real gap is the home hero and FAQ, and the quiz consent step.
4. The quiz already has a progress bar, Back, single-tap answers, contact details last and a "Book my call" button (T4). The real gaps are a numeric step label, an outcome-named submit button and a pre-submit review.
5. Company name, registration number and address are in `terms.html` AND `privacy.html`, but in no footer (T6, S3, C7). Two internal notes that said "only in terms.html" are wrong.
6. "2 to 4 times salary" has a current primary source after all: the Sanlam Benchmark (2025 and 2026 editions) puts employer death-benefit averages at roughly 2.8 to 3.4 times salary, and "about three times" for 2026 (C5). That does NOT support "Most work cover stops at 2 to 4x salary", because many people have no employer cover and funds range from 1x to 5x or more. Sanlam is an insurer, so the ARB independence rule (clauses 4.1.3 and 4.1.4) still needs checking. The employer-gap angle stays on hold.
7. The consumer opt-out blocks on the NCC national registry can already be registered (TechCentral update 9 Oct 2026); what starts later is marketer cleansing (Dec 2026 to Apr 2027) and enforcement (C9). The Terms sentence still needs rewording, but not for the reason first given.

**Search and AI visibility**
8. A Google Business Profile is off the launch gate (S2, CONFIRMED). Lead-generation companies and online-only businesses are ineligible.
9. FAQ rich results have not appeared for any site since 7 May 2026 (S6, CONFIRMED). FAQPage markup earns nothing; the visible FAQ still helps readers.
10. Google's AI-feature eligibility has one more condition than the research said: the site must not have opted out in Search Console's "Search generative AI" setting (default Include) (G1).
11. "Allow the five retrieval crawlers" is the right action, but "needs" is overstated: bingbot is not documented for ChatGPT, Perplexity or Claude, and Claude's reported backend is Brave, which will not crawl what Googlebot cannot (G2).
12. Vercel's AI Bots ruleset on Deny cuts live retrieval but does not "silently remove" the brand from answers, and a custom-rule bypass can allow answer-engine bots (G3).
13. "GEO is a brand-verification channel, not a lead channel" is an inference, not a finding (G6). AI referrals are about 0.3% of traffic on global averages; conversion quality is unproven either way.

**Measurement and testing**
14. At our traffic almost no page A/B test is powerable (U2, CONFIRMED). The repo rule "200 conversions per arm or 14 days" detects only a 27 to 29% relative lift at best. The spec moves experimentation to the ad level and reserves page tests for structural changes.
15. The 18% page-conversion KPI is Unbounce's insurance median for its own customers, not a like-for-like target (U1, CONFIRMED). The 20.6% "top performer" figure belongs to investing, not insurance (T4).
16. Search Console and CrUX will not show the speed Meta in-app visitors see (U5, CONFIRMED). A first-party RUM beacon is needed.

**Meta and tracking**
17. Business-messaging (click-to-WhatsApp) CAPI accepts `LeadSubmitted` and `QualifiedLead`, not `Lead` or `Schedule` (P4, CONFIRMED).
18. Event priority and AEM ranking were removed in May 2023 (P6, CONFIRMED); delete that launch step.
19. The "separate optional" ad-measurement consent is not separate or optional in the built code (P1). The spec splits it.
20. Meta imposes no South Africa-specific consent rule but defers to local law; that is not a safe harbour (P2). Combined with the Regulator's Dec 2024 listing of "use of cookies" under section 69, the conservative default is Pixel-after-opt-in, switchable by one flag once the attorney answers.
21. The opt-out must be a cookie on the registrable domain, because localStorage will not follow a visitor across subdomains (P3, CONFIRMED).
22. No special ad category or financial-services advertiser verification applies to South Africa on current Meta pages (P11), and conversion-quality optimisation for website leads exists only through unofficially documented sequenced events (P10).
23. A `wa.me` chat does NOT open the free entry-point window (that is for Click-to-WhatsApp ads), and Cloud API business replies in the service window are chargeable from 1 Oct 2026 after 1,000 free service messages per number per month (U10).

**Subdomains and hosting**
24. n8n's Webhook CORS option takes exact origins, not a regex (D8). Every live campaign origin must be listed, or CORS answered by the proxy.
25. Scope the strict apex CSP by negation (`missing` a campaign host), not by `has` apex host, so previews and the vercel.app host keep it (D5). Header rules match the incoming path, not the resolved one (D5, correcting the research).
26. `www` is already pointed at Vercel (a redirect), so "reserved labels are never pointed at Vercel" is wrong for that one label (D10). The Vercel plan is still unknown and Pro ($20 a month) is needed before paid traffic (D4, CONFIRMED).

**Motion**
27. Motion's AI Kit is not "$399 a year": Motion+ Solo is a US$399 one-time payment, and AI Kit is part of it (U4, M2). Motion core is free and MIT; nothing in this funnel needs Motion+.
28. The official Motion sizes (2.3 KB mini, 18 KB hybrid) are NOT brotli: only hybrid matches. Use measured brotli figures (M3).
29. Vanilla `animate()` stopped honouring `prefers-reduced-motion` in motion 13.3.0 (it did up to 13.2.0). Gate every call yourself (M4).
30. Fading the H1 in from opacity 0 can leave Chrome with no LCP candidate until a repaint. The safe rule is that nothing in the first viewport starts hidden or JS-gated (S4, U4, M8). A fade of below-the-fold content is fine.
31. Animating `height` to `'auto'` snaps at the midpoint in `motion/mini`; use an explicit pixel height or CSS (M9, M12). Several of the "five interactions that justify Motion" are close to free in native WAAPI or CSS, so Motion earns its bytes on about two to five (M9).
