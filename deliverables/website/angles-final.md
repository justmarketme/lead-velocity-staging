# SortMyCover: the final angle set (v1, 11 Oct 2026)

Status: PROPOSAL for Jonathan. Not legal advice. Nothing here is published, uploaded to Meta or DNS-attached. The compliance practitioner (Q1 to Q8 in `site-spec.md` L.2) and an attorney for the wills items sign off before any ad runs.

Inputs read: `research/ad-library-angles.md` and `research/ad-library/ad-catalog.csv` (Meta Ad Library, South Africa, collected 10 Oct 2026), `research/angles-desk-research.md`, `site-spec.md` sections C and I (rules S1 to S35), `creative-strategist/angle-ranking.md` and `concepts.md`, `media-buyer/campaign-spec.md`.

## 0. Decisions applied (from Jonathan)

- Scope: long-term insurance (life, disability, income protection, severe illness), **excluding funeral cover**, **plus** estate planning and drafting a will.
- Audience: age 35 to 50, budget R1,500+ preferred (R750+ qualifies); business owners, directors, professionals, parents, bond holders.
- Angles must be working now in the market (Library longevity, variant counts, white space), broker-neutral and compliant: no product names, premiums, comparisons, guarantees, "free" claims, fear framing or unsourced statistics. Only dated, sourced facts (the ASISA figure with source and month). "Authorised adviser" wording. SortMyCover never says it checks, sorts or arranges cover.
- **Dropped**: extended-family, funeral-led, price-led or discount, "free will", health-declined inclusion, fear or intestacy-state framing, anything unsourced.

## 1. How to read the evidence (and its limit)

The Ad Library shows no spend, impressions or results for South African commercial ads. Longevity ("started running on" to 10 Oct 2026) and the number of near-identical copies are proxies for "probably not losing money", nothing stronger. The Library rows below come from `ad-catalog.csv`; "Library #n" is the rank in `ad-library-angles.md` (Theme A or B tables). Desk grades (A to D) are from `angles-desk-research.md`. **Nobody has published a cost-per-qualified-lead ranking of angles for life cover; every ranking here is a hypothesis to test**, and at about 9 leads a week it cannot be settled by cost per lead (section 6).

Flags used below: **Green** = host now (standing gates Q1 and Q5 still apply before any ad); **Amber** = hosted, but the named item must be cleared before the first ad runs; **Hold** = the page is built but not hosted and gets no DNS, because the attorney or practitioner has to decide what we may promise at all (this is how `employer-gap` was treated).

## 2. Ranked angles

Ranking logic: Library breadth and longevity first, then white space for the 35 to 50, R1,500+ audience, then desk score, then compliance risk (higher risk ranks lower inside its theme).

## 6. Test plan for about 9 leads a week (all thresholds are PROPOSALS)

**Why not cost per lead:** at about 9 leads a week (about 36 a 30-day cycle), a 15:8 split between two angles gives p = 0.21 and a true 2x gap is only detected about 80% of the time when the weaker arm has about 20 leads (desk research, section 8, my calculation). Six ads in one cycle is about 6 leads each. So judge angles on upstream signals and pool by family before any cost-per-lead call.

**Cycle 1 ad set (one ad set; four distinct concepts, one execution each; different families so Meta sees them as distinct):**
1. Trigger: `new-bond`
2. Income: `salary-stops`
3. Identity: `owners-directors`
4. Conversation: `adviser-conversation`

Two executions of one concept are one concept (do not count colour pairs as a test). `cover-gap` waits for Q-G1 and the filed ASISA copy, then enters in cycle 2 as the "gap" concept. `new-baby` is the refresh pool. Theme B concepts enter only after Q-W1 to Q-W4 are answered; the first to release is `will-myth` or `children-guardian`, which replaces the weakest cycle-1 concept, never adds a fifth.

**Layers of judgement:**
- **Layer 1, attention:** hook rate (3-second plays over impressions) and outbound CTR. Judge an ad only after about 3,000 impressions and at least 30 outbound clicks.
- **Layer 2, funnel:** landing view to quiz start (form-start), quiz start to submission, WhatsApp verification. Judge after at least 10 submissions per ad.
- **Layer 3, money:** cost per qualified lead, then per attended meeting. Judge only on **pooled angle families across cycles** (3 cycles is about 108 leads, about 27 per concept).

**Kill rules (proposed):**
- Immediate: two disapprovals of one ad for the same reason; comments reading the ad as a promise or product offer; a broker complaint that a lead was misled.
- Soft kill (pause, do not delete): after 3,000 impressions, outbound CTR under half the ad-set median; or R1,000 spent with no form submission.
- Never kill on lead count alone. No lead-cost verdict before R3,000 spent or 14 days.

**Scale rules (proposed):** budget is capped by the broker ladder, so "scale" means reallocation. Pause losers so delivery concentrates; carry the winning family into new executions next cycle. Raise budget only when a broker adds a cycle. A concept is "ahead" only if its cost per qualified lead is at least 2x better than the median and layers 1 and 2 agree; at 1.5x say "unclear".

**Order across cycles:** cycle 1 trigger vs income vs identity vs conversation; cycle 2 keep the top two families, add `cover-gap` and one more; cycle 3 executions (hook line, format) inside the winning family. Do not test colour or CTA wording before the family is known. No edits on days 1 to 2. Report weekly, decide fortnightly.
