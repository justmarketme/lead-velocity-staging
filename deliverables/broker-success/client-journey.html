05

The fine print, in plain words

What counts as a qualified lead

  1. Aged 35 to 50.
  2. Monthly budget of R750–R1,250, or R1,250 and up.
  3. A real SA mobile number, consent given, and they replied or tapped on WhatsApp within 72 hours.
  4. Happy to have a video, WhatsApp or phone call.
  5. Not a duplicate from the last 90 days.

Your post-meeting buttons

  • The price never depends on how many policies you write.
  • If we fall short, we keep delivering for up to 14 extra days, only for delays outside our control (day 44 at most). After that the balance rolls into your next cycle on top of its number, or you can ask for a refund at your plan's price per lead, paid within 7 working days.
  • Replacements are goodwill, not a right: up to 3 requests a calendar week on every plan, the Pilot included, for no-shows and leads we couldn't reach, with proof.
  • Delivered leads are yours to use exclusively, and are never sold to another broker.
