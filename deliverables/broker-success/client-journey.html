Your weekly report, and replacements

One short WhatsApp report every Monday. It shows delivered against committed, your show rate, lead quality, and the one thing we need from you that week.

You get

Replaced for you

When things don't go to plan

They don't book straight away

Three gentle nudges at 2 hours, 24 hours and 72 hours. Each adds one useful detail about the call. No discounts and no fake deadlines.

They don't show up

"No stress, things happen. Here are 3 new times." One offer, no guilt. If they don't rebook, the lead is replaced.

They need to move the time

They tap Reschedule in WhatsApp and pick a new free slot. Your Outlook updates on its own.

The fine print, in plain words

What counts as a qualified lead

  1. Aged 35 to 50.
  2. Monthly budget of R750–R1,250, or R1,250 and up.
  3. A real SA mobile number, consent given, and they replied or tapped on WhatsApp within 72 hours.
  4. Happy to have a video, WhatsApp or phone call.
  5. Not a duplicate from the last 90 days.
