# Broker onboarding film: "What happens next"

Voice: Azure/Edge neural **en-US-AndrewNeural** (house explainer voice). About 3 minutes. Lead Velocity brand, house design (pink to purple gradient, white cards, navy accents).
Facts are sourced in `research-ads.md`; prices come from `automation/billing/pricing.seed.json` (live plans as of 10 Oct 2026: Pilot, Bronze, Silver, Gold; Bronze is the worked example, as modelled in MASTER-PROMPT 3.5). Counting and replacement rules follow `src/lib/pricing.ts` and the portal.
Never claim results, guaranteed lookalikes, or anything tied to policies. "About" and "modelled" stay in.

## Scene 1: Welcome (about 15s)
**Say:** Welcome to Lead Velocity. You've paid for your first cycle, so here's exactly what happens next: how your leads reach you, where your money goes, and how the results get better over time.
**Show:** Lead Velocity logo. Title "What happens next". Three chips: Your leads · Your money · Getting better.

## Scene 2: Getting you live (about 25s)
**Say:** First, we get you set up. We check your FSP number on the public FSCA register. You connect your Microsoft Outlook calendar, add your photo and a short bio for your intro card, and give us your business WhatsApp number. Once your payment has cleared and that's done, your campaigns go live, normally within three business days.
**Show:** Onboarding checklist that ticks itself: FSP check · Calendar connected (Microsoft Outlook) · Photo and bio (your intro card) · WhatsApp number · Campaigns live (within 3 business days of payment and onboarding).

## Scene 3: How a lead reaches you (about 45s)
**Say:** Our ads run under our consumer brand, SortMyCover. They never name a product or a price. Someone taps an ad, answers a few quick questions, and agrees to be contacted. Our WhatsApp assistant follows up within minutes, introduces you by name, practice and FSP number, and books a time with you. The booking only appears in your calendar once the client has verified their email. You get a WhatsApp and a short pre-call brief. We send them reminders right up to ten minutes before the meeting. After that, the conversation is yours. A lead only counts as one of your Qualified Leads once the client has verified their email and you've actually met them, which you mark as Attended in your portal.
**Show:** Animated flow: Ad → Quick questions → Consent → WhatsApp follow-up → Time booked (not in your calendar yet) → Email verified (now it's in your calendar) → You get a WhatsApp + brief → Reminders → Your meeting → Marked Attended in your portal = Qualified Lead (counter ticks 1 of 20). Counter caption: "Counts once their email is verified and you mark Attended".

## Scene 4: Where your money goes (about 50s)
**Say:** There are four plans, each a flat fee for a set number of Qualified Leads. A once-off Pilot: ten for eight thousand five hundred rand. Bronze: twenty for sixteen thousand five hundred. Silver: thirty for twenty-four thousand five hundred. And Gold: forty-five for thirty-five thousand five hundred. Take Bronze. You pay sixteen thousand five hundred rand for a thirty-day cycle. We plan for just over half of that, about eight and a half thousand rand, to go straight into advertising on Meta. That's the biggest single part. The rest runs everything around the ads: the WhatsApp assistant, booking and reminders, the creative, the testing and tracking, and the people doing the work. It's a flat fee. It's never linked to policies, premiums or commission.
**Show:** Four plan cards: Pilot 10 leads R8,500 once-off · Bronze 20 leads R16,500 · Silver 30 leads R24,500 · Gold 45 leads R35,500 (Bronze highlighted as the example). Bar of R16,500 splitting into "Advertising on Meta, about R8,500 (planned)" and "Everything around the ads: WhatsApp assistant, booking and reminders, creative, testing, tracking, team". Footnote: "Prices excl. VAT. Planned split for Bronze. Flat fee, never linked to policies."

## Scene 5: How we keep improving (about 30s)
**Say:** We never set it and forget it. We test a few genuinely different ads side by side, usually for at least a week, before we pick a winner. We watch the numbers every day: cost per lead, how many people book, and how many actually show up. When people have seen an ad too often, results get more expensive, so we keep refreshing the creative. Meta's system usually needs about fifty results in a week to settle, so we make changes on purpose, not on impulse.
**Show:** Two ad cards A and B with a "1 week" timer, then a winner badge. Mini dashboard: Cost per lead · Booked · Attended. "About 50 results a week to settle" note.

## Scene 6: More data, better leads (about 35s)
**Say:** Here's what makes it better over time. When a person attends, and they've said yes to ad measurement, we tell Meta. That shows the system who your real clients look like, not just who clicked. Once we have at least a hundred of those people, Meta can build a lookalike audience: new people who share their characteristics. It works better with a thousand or more, which is why we pool what we learn across all the advisers we work with. At around twenty a month, this is a slow build, not a switch. Lookalikes are available for these ads in South Africa today, but Meta can change that, so we treat them as a bonus, never a promise. And we never target anyone by health, income or other sensitive traits.
**Show:** Dots filling a grid toward "100: lookalike possible" with a marker at "1,000+: works better". Small print: "Only people who ticked the ad-measurement box. Never targeted by health, income or other sensitive traits. Availability set by Meta."

## Scene 7: Your part (about 25s)
**Say:** Your part is simple. Keep your calendar up to date. After every meeting, tap Attended or No-show in your portal. That tap is the signal that improves your leads. If someone doesn't show, wait ten minutes, then send us your proof straight away. If you couldn't reach a lead, send us proof of that too. Out of goodwill, we replace up to three a week.
**Show:** Phone mock of the Lead Velocity portal: "Thandi M. · today's meeting. Did they attend?" with buttons [Attended] [No-show]. Checklist: Calendar current · One tap in your portal after every meeting · No-show or couldn't reach them: send proof (no-show: wait 10 min, then right away) · "Out of goodwill, we replace up to 3 a week".

## Scene 8: Close (about 10s)
**Say:** That's it. We'll handle the ads, the follow-up and the bookings. You do what you do best. Welcome to Lead Velocity.
**Show:** Logo, "howzit@leadvelocity.co.za", "Questions? Reply on WhatsApp any time."
