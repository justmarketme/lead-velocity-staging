Questions people ask

How long does it take?

The check takes about a minute. The call is 30 minutes, at a time you pick.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The call is free and there’s no obligation. Advisers pay us a flat fee to be introduced. We never earn commission on anything you buy, and nothing is added to your premium.

Who is the adviser?

A licensed financial adviser (an authorised financial services provider). You’ll see their name, practice and FSP number on WhatsApp before you speak to them, and you can check it on the FSCA register.

What happens to my information?

We share it with the adviser you’re introduced to, and no one else. You can ask us to delete it at any time. Reply STOP to any message and we stop. Our privacy notice has the detail.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. The call is to understand your numbers. Any decision is yours, with the adviser, afterwards.

Is SortMyCover an insurer?

No. We don’t sell cover, give advice, compare products or quote premiums. We connect you with a licensed adviser who can.
