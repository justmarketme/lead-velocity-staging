Common questions

What is the cover gap?

Many people have life cover through work. Most work cover is a set multiple of yearly salary, often only a few times what a person earns in a year. Many bonds and other debts are bigger than that. The difference between what a family would need and what the cover pays is the cover gap.

What happens on a 30-minute call with a licensed adviser?

The adviser asks about the family, the bond and any cover already in place. They explain the options in plain words and answer questions. The call is by video, WhatsApp or phone. There is no obligation to buy anything.

What does SortMyCover do, and not do?

SortMyCover connects people with authorised financial services providers and helps book the call. We do not give financial advice, compare products or quote premiums. Any advice comes from the licensed adviser, not from us.
