2. What we do

  • We ask you a few short questions. They check that a call with an adviser suits you.
  • If it suits you, and you agree, we pass your details to one adviser. The adviser is named when you agree, or in our first WhatsApp to you. That message shows their name, practice and FSP number.
  • We book your call and send you reminders on WhatsApp.

Your free cover check is a conversation with that adviser. It is a call of about 30 minutes.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not give financial advice.
  • We do not compare products, quote premiums or sell policies.
  • Our WhatsApp assistant uses AI. It can answer questions about the call and help you book. It never gives advice. Type “person” at any time to reach a human.
  • What you read on this site is general information. It is not advice. Only a licensed adviser can tell you what fits you.

4. It is free, and you do not have to buy

You pay us nothing. You do not have to buy anything, on the call or after it.

Advisers pay us a flat fee for each 30-day cycle. The fee is the same whether or not you buy. We earn nothing from any policy. See how we make money.

Your adviser is an authorised financial services provider. They run their own business. They are not our employee or agent. They are responsible for any advice they give you and any product they offer. They must give you their own disclosures on the call.
