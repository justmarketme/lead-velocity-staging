What happens on a 30-minute call with a licensed adviser

Written by the SortMyCover editorial team. Updated 2 October 2026.

A SortMyCover call is a short chat with an authorised financial services provider, called a licensed adviser. It is free and there is no obligation. Here is what usually happens.

Before the call

The person picks a time that suits them. The call can be by video, WhatsApp or phone. The adviser shares their name and licence details first, so the person can check them.

What the adviser asks

  • Who depends on the person's income
  • What the bond and other debts look like
  • What cover the person already has, including cover through work
  • What the person can comfortably budget each month

The adviser asks these questions to understand the family. There are no trick questions.

What the adviser explains

The adviser explains the options in plain words and answers questions. They also explain how they are paid.

What the adviser does not do

  • No pressure to decide on the call
  • No obligation to buy anything
  • No need to share bank card details on the call

The person can say no, or ask for time to think.

What SortMyCover does

SortMyCover helps book the call and then steps back. We do not join the call. Read how SortMyCover works and about the life cover gap.

Common questions

Does the call cost anything?

No. The call is free for the person who asks for it. Advisers pay SortMyCover a flat fee. Read how it works on this site.

Do I have to buy something after the call?

No. There is no obligation. The adviser can explain the options and the person can decide later, or not at all.
