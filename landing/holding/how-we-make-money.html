How SortMyCover makes money

Short answer: advisers pay us a flat fee. People who ask for a call pay nothing.

What advisers pay

A licensed adviser pays Lead Velocity a flat fee for each 30-day cycle. In return, they get a set number of people who asked for a call and said they are open to one. The fee is the same whether or not anyone buys a product.

What we never take

  • No commission, ever.
  • No share of any premium.
  • No fee from the person who asks for a call.

Why this matters

Because the fee is flat, SortMyCover has no reason to push anyone toward a product. We also do not give advice, compare products or quote premiums. That is the adviser's job, and the adviser explains how they are paid.
