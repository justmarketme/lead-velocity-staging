What we do

We explain, in plain words, what a cover gap is and why many people have one. When a person asks for it, we connect them with an authorised financial services provider, called a licensed adviser, and help book a 30-minute call. The call is by video, WhatsApp or phone.

What we do not do

We connect you with authorised financial services providers. We do not give financial advice, compare products or quote premiums.

Only the licensed adviser can talk about products. The adviser gives their name and licence details before the call.
