Complaints

If something went wrong, tell us. A person will reply within 48 hours.

How to complain

  • Send a message to SortMyCover on Facebook Messenger.
  • Or send the word COMPLAINT on WhatsApp in any SortMyCover chat.

What to include

Your name, the number you used, and a short note on what happened.

What happens next

We log every complaint, reply within 48 hours and tell you what we will do. Complaints about an adviser's advice or products can also go to that adviser's firm and to the regulator.
