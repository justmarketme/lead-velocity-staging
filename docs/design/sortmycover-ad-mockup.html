Facebook Feed · 4:5 static or 6-second motion

Why it looks like this

  • The number is the hook. “2–4× salary” sits in the first line of both the image and the primary text. Finance ads with a concrete figure in the first 3 seconds hooked 31% above category average. Meta 2025 benchmark
  • Third person throughout. “Most work life cover…”, never “your cover is too small”. That keeps it inside Meta’s personal-attributes policy and off the FAIS advice line. 2.1.8
  • Payslip and bond statement, not a stock family. The gap is shown, not claimed. The amounts on the paper are illustrative props, and no premium or cover figure is ever promised. 4D.4a
  • Amber on charcoal. Every SA insurer in this feed is blue or green. Amber is the one warm colour that pops against Facebook’s own blue-white UI, and it isn’t owned by anyone in the category. Romaniuk: ~4% of brands own a colour
  • The tick is the logo. One device, drawn as the “o” in Cover, legible at avatar size. No shield, umbrella or silhouette. Logos rank highest for recall
  • The CTA says “check”, never “quote”. The destination is a conversation with a licensed adviser, and the footer line is the same seven words on every asset. Tagline rule
  • “AI-generated imagery” in small type. Not required in SA, but it costs nothing and reads as honest. 4D.1

Why frame 1 is built this way

  • Text on screen at 0.0 seconds. Over 80% of plays are sound-off. Text in the first half-second lifts hook rate 4–9 points. AdLibrary audit data
  • A face, close up, eye contact. The single strongest opener in the data, +4–10 points. This is an AI-rendered everyday South African, never presented as a client or adviser. The real adviser appears only in the WhatsApp intro video. Meta AI rules
  • Payoff promised in frame 1. “Read this before the champagne” sets up the reveal, delivered by second 6. +5–12 pts
  • Burned-in caption doubles as the spoken line, so the sound-off viewer and the sound-on viewer see the same beat.
  • Amber highlight on the key phrase is the brand asset doing the work inside a vertical video where the logo is tiny.
  • Life event as the entry point. New bond is one of the five moments the brand is tied to. The creative selects the audience, not the age slider. Sharp · Andromeda
