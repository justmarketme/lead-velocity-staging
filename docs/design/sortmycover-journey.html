Why each message exists

W06 · < 60 sIntro card + disclosureThe adviser, practice and FSP number land in the first message — the one people actually read. Message ID and delivery status are stored as disclosure evidence. HBR: contact within the hour ≈ 7× qualification odds.
W07 · LLMCommitment askAsking her to type the date back is the NHS commitment effect (−18% missed appointments when people write it themselves). Thandi discloses she's an AI on first contact.
W09 · T0 + 10What to expectUncertainty is the main no-show driver. Three bullets, one .ics, no selling.
W07 · guardrailThe cost questionThe reply model drafted something about premiums; the classifier gate caught it and swapped in the deferral line. The question is now in Mark's pre-call brief. Lerato gets a warm, human answer and nothing that counts as advice.
W09 · T-48 hMark's 26-second videoRecorded by Mark in the portal from an AI-written script, captions burned in, lower-third carries the FSP. The text above it is personalised; the video is not — no voice cloning.
W09 · T-24 hConfirm + positive norm"Most people find 30 minutes is all it takes" is the social-norm line from the same NHS study (combined effect −31.7%). Prep nudge is useful, optional, and signals seriousness.
W09 · T-2 h / T-10Specific-cost framing"Mark has set aside 30 minutes for you" is true and specific (PLOS ONE RCTs: specific beats vague). Link twice so nobody hunts for it.
W12 · T+30Two-sided outcomeLerato is asked too, so a broker no-show can't be logged as a lead no-show. Then we go quiet: the adviser owns every follow-up from here (FAIS).

Why the broker side looks like this

Pre-call brief is the headline featureMark walks in knowing the person: bands only (no sensitive data), her questions in her own words, and what she cared about. Conversica/Verse call this the hand-off moment; it's the thing brokers tell other brokers about.
One tap closes the loopOutcome capture at T+15 with buttons, nudge at +3 h, auto-attended at 24 h if unmarked. Replacements are goodwill, at our discretion: up to 3 requests a Calendar Week, no-shows and unreachable leads together, each with proof.
His calendar, not oursThe Teams meeting sits in Mark's own Outlook via Microsoft Graph. Lerato is not an attendee (keeps his email private); she gets her own .ics and link.
Nothing from us after “Attended”Quotes, advice and follow-up are Mark's — that's what keeps Lead Velocity on the right side of FAIS and what makes the flat fee defensible.
