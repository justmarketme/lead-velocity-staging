# Wills and estate sources (18 Oct 2026)

On 18 Oct 2026, Jono decided that SortMyCover does not name or rely on its will provider in public. The provider is named only inside WhatsApp, before the call. The wills and estate guides therefore rest on South African government and statutory sources. Every page below was opened on 18 Oct 2026, and the evidence ids are in `config/evidence.json`.

SAFLII and lawlibrary.org.za returned 403 to automated fetches. Statute text comes from gov.za PDFs and, for the Wills Act, the consolidated text on acts.co.za. The gov.za Wills Act PDF is the unamended 1953 text.

## Cautions recorded by the research

- **Signing rule.** The Master's wills page says to sign "every other page". The consolidated Act, section 2(1)(a)(iv), says each page other than the last. The guides follow the Act.
- **Magistrate's service points.** The R125 000 figure on the Master page conflicts with an unverified R150 000 in a DOJ pamphlet. The guides do not state either figure.
- **Outdated pages.** The Guardian's Fund page still cites the repealed Child Care Act. The Master FAQ's interest table stops at 2020/21, so no Guardian's Fund interest rate is published.
- **Not verified.**
  - The life-partner amendment to the Maintenance of Surviving Spouses Act (Act 15 of 2023) is not on gov.za, so it is not stated.
  - The 21-day inspection period in section 35 of the Administration of Estates Act is not stated.
- **No commercial sources.** Will-drafting companies are never used as sources.
