2. What we do

  • We ask you two short questions (an age band and a budget band), then your first name and mobile number.
  • If it suits you, and you agree, we pass your details to one adviser. The adviser is named when you agree, or in our first WhatsApp to you. That message shows their name, practice and FSP number.
  • We book your call and send you reminders on WhatsApp.

The call is with that adviser. It is about 30 minutes, booked for a time you pick. The call costs you nothing.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not give financial advice.
  • We do not compare products, quote premiums or sell policies.
  • Our WhatsApp assistant uses AI. It can answer questions about the call and help you book. It never gives advice. Type “person” at any time to reach a human.
  • What you read on this site is general information. It is not advice. Only a licensed adviser can tell you what fits you.

4. The call costs you nothing, and you do not have to buy

You pay us nothing. You do not have to buy anything, on the call or after it.

Advisers pay SortMyCover a fee for the service. The fee does not depend on whether you buy anything. SortMyCover takes no commission and no share of any premium. See how we make money.

5. Your adviser

Your adviser is an authorised financial services provider. They run their own business. They are not our employee or agent. They are responsible for any advice they give you and any product they offer. They must give you their own disclosures on the call.

6. You can stop at any time

  • Reply STOP to any WhatsApp, or email hello@sortmycover.co.za with the word STOP. We stop at once and tell the adviser.
  • You can cancel or move your call at any time. Use the buttons in our WhatsApp, or reply to us.
  • You can also register with the National Consumer Commission opt-out registry. Its enforcement and bulk effect start between December 2026 and May 2027. Once it applies to us, we will check our records against it and a registered block will win, even if you agreed before.
