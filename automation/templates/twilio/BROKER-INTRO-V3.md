# broker_intro_slots_v3: for Jono with the Instant Form launch

**Status: proposal, NOT submitted.** The intro card a lead-ad (Instant Form) lead gets from W06, with the SortMyCover disclosure that `/start/` (W39 `START-DISCLOSE-v1`) and the CTWA chat show. Same category (UTILITY), header (intro card image), variables and buttons as `broker_intro_slots`; the body adds the three disclosure sentences and drops "Our WhatsApp assistant uses AI." Files: `automation/templates/broker_intro_slots_v3.json` (Meta spec, `HELD` in `templates/submit.sh`) and `content/broker_intro_slots_v3.json` (Twilio Content, generated by `build-content.mjs`).

Hi {{1}}, thanks for your insurance and financial planning enquiry. Your details have been passed to *{{2}} (FSP {{3}})*, an authorised financial services provider. *{{4}}* can do a 30-minute call. Pick a time below.

Variables: 1 first name · 2 practice (Oracle Private Wealth) · 3 FSP number (28418) · 4 adviser name · 5-7 slot labels; header = the intro card under `TWILIO_MEDIA_BASE_URL`. Footer "SortMyCover". Quick replies: Time 1 · Time 2 · Time 3 · Other times. No "free", no "AI", no "Lead Velocity"; `node automation/templates/check.mjs` passes (the disclosure sentence is the approved site footer wording, allowed by name).

## Submit (Jono's gate)

```

node automation/templates/twilio/submit-twilio.mjs --only broker_intro_slots_v3 --submit   # creates the Content + WhatsApp approval request
```

Then add `"broker_intro_slots_v3": "HX..."` to `TWILIO_CONTENT_SIDS` (n8n env) once the approval status is `approved`.

## What changes when the SID is present

- W06 sends `broker_intro_slots_v3` instead of `broker_intro_slots` to **lead-ad** leads only (`lib/w06.mjs planFirstTouch`, `ctx.intro_v3` = `lib/w01.mjs introV3Ready`: `WHATSAPP_PROVIDER=twilio` and the key in `TWILIO_CONTENT_SIDS`). CTWA, /start/ and page leads keep their cards.
- W01 Lead core stops sending the interim disclosure text (below), because the card carries it.

## Until then (live once W01 and W06 are imported)

On the lead's band tap (its first reply: the 24-h session is open), W01 Lead core (`route_and_first_touch`, called by W03 finishLeadAd) finds the broker `routingFor` would pick and, before routing, sends this session text through smc-whatsapp-send (waits), then writes `lead_activities` `adviser_disclosed` (`LEADAD-DISCLOSE-v1`, key `w01:disclosed:<lead>:<broker>`, only with a message id) and only then routes:

> Before I introduce you: {adviser name} from {practice}, an authorised financial services provider (FSP {fsp}), will be the one on the call with you. Introduced by SortMyCover. SortMyCover gives no financial advice, product comparisons or premium quotes. Advisers pay SortMyCover a fee that does not depend on whether you buy anything.

`routeExisting` refuses a lead-ad lead without that row for its broker (action `disclose`); a failed send holds it (`held_disclosure_failed`). Nobody can take the lead: no text (the held path sends no intro). Known gap: if W06 falls back to the Flow card (`broker_intro_slots_v2`, fewer than 3 free slots) while v3 is configured, that card has no disclosure.
